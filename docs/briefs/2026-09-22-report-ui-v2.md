## What the owner said about today's report (2026-09-22)

"If I had to craft in the game by this instruction it is terribly awkward and clumsy. The instruction should borrow
from the game as much as possible and show short examples. Half of the UX is filler for the sake of filler." The look
they like is the new Craft of Exile (beta.craftofexile.com): the item as an in-game tooltip, mod changes marked
Added / Removed, a simulator flow of step boxes with currency icons and routes with conditions, and a "Costs per
success" table. Words are the game's own (they play in English): "Prefix modifier", "Tier", "+(130-144) to maximum
Life", "Deafening Essence of Envy" -- never a translation.

## The new page, top to bottom

1. **Target item** -- an in-game tooltip card (dark, the rarity colour header, base name, item level, then each
   target mod the way the game's advanced tooltip shows it: `Prefix modifier (Tier: 1) -- Life` over
   `+(130-144) to maximum Life`; "one of" alternatives as one card line listing the options).
2. **Routes** -- a compact list, best first, at most 6 visible (the rest behind "show all"). Each row: the route as a
   chain of currency chips (name + a small colour mark per currency family; icons are a later step), "Success chance
   per try: 1 out of N (x%)" where the route has one repeated action, average cost and p90 in Chaos Orb with a chaos
   chip. Clicking a row selects it for sections 3-4. A row whose p90 label is "approximate" gets a "~" before p90,
   nothing more.
3. **Crafting flow of the selected route** -- like Craft of Exile's simulator flow: step boxes left to right
   (`Step 1` title, the currency chip, the expected number of uses), arrows between them labelled with the condition
   in game words ("has +(130-144) to maximum Life and one of +(46-48)% Fire / Cold / Lightning Resistance"), a
   "repeat" loop arrow on a step that is spammed, a flag at the success end, a house where the route restarts from a
   new base. Under each box: chance per use, average uses, cost of the step. Inline SVG or HTML/CSS, no library.
4. **Step by step, like doing it in game** -- a numbered list of at most one line per step in the game's verbs:
   "Use Deafening Essence of Envy on a normal Royal Burgonet (ilvl 86). Repeat until the item has ...". Beside the
   list, ONE example item after the key step: the tooltip card with the hit mods marked Added (green) and what a
   miss typically looks like marked Removed (red) -- the engine's own sample, never invented text.
5. **Costs per success** -- the materials table like Craft of Exile's: currency chip | used (average) | chaos each |
   chaos total; one line total. Base cost shown if the route consumes bases.
6. Footer: "Prices are placeholders, not trade prices" (one line), a link to `evidence.html`, the generator version.

## Style

Dark page like the Craft of Exile beta: near-black background, dark panels with a thin gold-brown frame, headings in
a serif small-caps face (Google Fonts only, e.g. Cinzel or similar; body in a plain sans), item tooltip colours:
normal white, magic blue, rare yellow, mod text blue-white, Added green, Removed red. Follow the page contract of the
parent repo's artifact rules only as far as they make sense for a local file: colours as CSS variables, readable at
1280 px and at a phone width (the flow scrolls horizontally inside its own box, the page never does).

## Rules

- Game data and every number come from the engine and its data files; no game fact typed from memory.
- Nothing outside `crafting/` is touched. No network use at generation time (fonts may load from Google Fonts in the
  browser; the page must still read without them).
- Durations for a human: seconds under ~90 s, then minutes, then hours and minutes.
