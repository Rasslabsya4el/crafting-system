## 0.2.0 — Dawn of the Hunt (2025-04-04)

Release: 2025-04-04 12:00 PDT; league "Dawn of the Hunt" (Standard/HC/SSF). — search snippets; https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3740562

- **Fracturing Orb added:** "The Fracturing Orb fractures a random modifier on a rare item with at least 4 modifiers, locking it in place." Guaranteed drop from Cleansed bosses per Maxroll. — https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3740562 ; https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/patch-0-2-0-dawn-of-the-hunt-reveal-megapost
- **Recombination added to Expedition:** "A new crafting mechanic called Recombination has been added to Expedition"; chance falls as more mods are kept; "If you are not successful, the items will be destroyed."; fuelled by Expedition artifacts. — https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3740562
- **Corrupted Essences:** "Essences can now have Vaal Orbs applied to them to corrupt the Essences with a range of unpredictable outcomes." and "Greater Essences are possible to be upgraded to four new Corrupted Essences." (Hysteria, Delirium, Horror, Insanity per Maxroll). — https://poe2db.tw/us/Version_0.2.0 ; https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/patch-0-2-0-dawn-of-the-hunt-reveal-megapost
- "Patrolling monsters can no longer be imprisoned in Essences." — https://poe2db.tw/us/Version_0.2.0
- **Vaal Orb:** "Using a Vaal Orb on an item can no longer cause a modifier with a value of 1 to be reduced to 0." — https://poe2db.tw/us/Version_0.2.0
- **Jeweller's Orbs** now *set* a gem's support-socket count instead of adding one ("Jeweller's Orbs now set a Skill Gem to have the specified number of Support Gem Sockets"). — https://poe2db.tw/us/Version_0.2.0
- **Runes:** "Runes now have 3 tiers" (Lesser / normal / Greater); 3 Lesser reforge to 1 normal and 3 normal to 1 Greater; "You can no longer reforge 3 Runes to obtain a random Rune in return."; "Doubled the drop rate of Runes." — https://poe2db.tw/us/Version_0.2.0
- Some Rune and Soul Core modifiers removed or lowered (max Life, max Mana, all-Attributes removed); "Added 7 new random per Socketed Rune or Soul Core modifiers." — https://poe2db.tw/us/Version_0.2.0
- **Talismans** introduced (drop from Azmeri wisps). — https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/patch-0-2-0-dawn-of-the-hunt-reveal-megapost **[secondary]**
- **Omens:** "Omens now always appear as Ritual Rewards in Map areas. There's also a small chance for an additional Omen to appear." Names of any new 0.2.0 omens (e.g. Omen of Recombination) were not in the readable part of the notes **[unverified which omens were added in 0.2.0]**. — https://poe2db.tw/us/Version_0.2.0
- **Mod pool:** Belts/Gloves/Boots max Mana capped at "+105-124"; Rings "+165-179"; Sceptres "+4 to Level of all Minion Skills (previously +5)"; Physical Thorns mods buffed up to 3.5x; "Increased Rarity of Items found Modifiers on items have been lowered at top end." — https://poe2db.tw/us/Version_0.2.0
- **Corruption:** Helmet/Ring "1-2% of Life Regenerated per Second" corruption mod can no longer roll; Jewel attribute corruption now "+4-6". — https://poe2db.tw/us/Version_0.2.0
- **Instilling:** some starting-area notables can no longer be instilled; existing ones keep working. — https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3740562
- **Waystones:** "Waystones can no longer drop at a lower tier than the map you are in."; Corrupted Nexus needs a Waystone with "at least 4 modifiers"; unidentified Waystones give "30% increased Waystone drop chance, instead of 30% increased Quantity." — https://poe2db.tw/us/Version_0.2.0
- "The Reforging Benches in towns have been repositioned to more optimal locations." — https://poe2db.tw/us/Version_0.2.0

- **Essence rework:** "Essences now have 4 tiers: Lesser, Normal, Greater, and Perfect."; "Essences now add a specific modifier, depending on the Tier of Essence and type of item it is being applied to."; Lesser/Normal/Greater upgrade Magic to Rare with a guaranteed mod; "Perfect Essences and Essences obtained through Corruption remove a random modifier" and add a guaranteed one to a Rare; 7 new Essence types; Torment renamed Abrasion; Hysteria varies by item type. — https://poe2db.tw/us/Version_0.3.0 ; https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3826682

## 0.5.0 — Return of the Ancients, league "Runes of Aldur" (2026-05-29)

Notes posted 2026-05-22; first hotfixes 2026-05-30, launch 2026-05-29 (PDT) per news. — https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540 ; search snippet (pcgamesn/u4n: "revealed on May 7th and launched on May 29th, 2026")

- **Recombinator removed:** "The Recombinator has been disabled."; "The Omen of Recombination has been removed. Existing Omens of Recombination will be deleted upon logging in." — https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540
- **Crafted-mod rule:** "All crafted modifiers are now guaranteed, but items can only have 1 crafted modifier at a time." — https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540
- **Desecrated limit:** "Desecrated modifiers no longer count as crafted modifiers, but items are limited to 1 Desecrated modifier." — https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540
- **Currency rarity:** "Greater and Perfect currencies have been made somewhat rarer", "Transmutation and Augmentation being made significantly rarer"; Greater Transmutation/Augmentation "now have a minimum Modifier Level of 44 (previously 55)" and drop from Act 4; "Divine Orbs are now more common." — https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540
- **Corruption and Sanctification:** corruption's value outcome now "multiplies each modifier based on the current value, instead of randomising the value"; "Sanctifying an item now multiplies each modifier value based on the current value, instead of randomising values." — https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540
- **Essences:** more common in the campaign but "the proportion of these being Essences of the Infinite has been significantly decreased"; Perfect Essence of Battle +3 two-hand/crossbow, +2 one-hand/bow; Hysteria on Foci ES recharge 20-23% (was 41-45%). — https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540 ; https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/0-5-0-patch-notes-return-of-the-ancients
- **Alloys (new, 13):** add crafted mods "by replacing an existing modifier, similar to Perfect Essences"; item text "Removes a random modifier and augments a Rare item with a new guaranteed modifier." (Runic, Adaptive, Protective, Expansive, Swift, Cyclonic, Prismatic, Mystic, Sovereign, Celestial, Transcendent, Runebinder's, Runefather's). — https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540 ; https://poe2db.tw/us/Stackable_Currency
- **Fluxes (new):** 3 resist-conversion Fluxes from Remnants (today's list: Blazing, Chilling, Crackling, Void; plus Thaumaturgic and Perfect Flux for skill levels). — https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540 ; https://poe2db.tw/us/Stackable_Currency

## Conflicts and open points

1. **1.0 date:** the press release says 2026-12-11 PST; the interview summary on poe2db says "launches on December 12" (Korean host's time). Same moment in different time zones, most likely. — https://poe2db.tw/us/Version_1.0.0
2. **"Crafted modifier" in 0.5.0:** a third-party summary claims "any crafting method that guarantees a modifier counts as a crafted modifier" (Essences, Alloys, etc.). The official text only says "All crafted modifiers are now guaranteed, but items can only have 1 crafted modifier at a time." Which methods' mods count as "crafted" (Perfect/Corrupted Essences? Alloys? Liquid Emotions on jewels?) is **not confirmed** and matters for planning. — https://bugfree.gg/guides/poe2-crafting-changes-0-5-0 (search snippet) vs https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540
3. **Greater Transmutation/Augmentation minimum level:** 55 before 0.5.0, 44 after (official). Perfect stays 70 (current data). Any data snapshot from before 2026-05-29 carries 55.
4. **Transcendent Alloy on Foci/Wands:** removed in 0.5.2, restored at lower values in 0.5.3. — https://poe2db.tw/us/Version_0.5.2 ; https://poe2db.tw/us/Version_0.5.3
5. **Which omens came in 0.2.0 vs 0.3.0:** the "15 new Omens" of 0.3.0 are not named in the readable notes; Omen of Recombination came with Recombination (0.2.0) **[assumed]**. Sanctification's introduction patch is not found.
6. **Fate of the Vaal crafting rules:** the 0.4.0 notes do not describe the temple rooms; the descriptions come from poe2db and news. Vaal Infuser before 0.5.0 (one item for all weapons/armour) is from a search snippet only.
7. **0.5.0 launch date:** 2026-05-29 per news; the forum thread holds the notes from 2026-05-22 and hotfixes from 2026-05-30. Consistent, but not read from an official launch post.
8. **Hotfix gaps:** 0.1.0a–d, 0.1.1b–f, 0.2.0b–f, 0.3.0b and later, 0.4.0c–k, 0.5.0b, 0.5.4b–f and 0.5.5g were not read for crafting. The official forum (view-forum/2212, about 50 pages) is reachable and is the place to close these.
9. **Wikis blocked:** poe2wiki.net "Version history" and per-version pages could not be read (Anubis bot wall), so no cross-check against the wiki was done.
10. **0.1.0 baseline:** no primary text listing the launch crafting rules was read.
