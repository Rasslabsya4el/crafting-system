## Answer

- **Six influenced Exalted Orbs.** Shaper's and Elder's (since 3.26.0) and Crusader's, Hunter's, Redeemer's and Warlord's (since 3.9.0). Each adds its influence and one explicit mod of that influence to a **rare item that has no influence at all** (not even the same one), is not fractured, synthesised or carrying an Eldritch implicit, and has item level 68 or more (below that the pool is empty), with an open slot. Each orb adds a mod of its influence; the six differ only in the influence.
- **The drawn mod** comes from that influence's mods only (never the ordinary pool), through the shared add: item level, family exclusion, side capacity, spawn weight, `Cannot roll Attack/Caster Modifiers` and open-slot rules as for an Exalted Orb. Catalyst quality does not change the weights (3.15.0). The pools are 138 (class, influence) pairs, 23 classes x 6; every pair has prefixes and suffixes at item level 100 (table below).
- **Two-influence limit.** An item has one influence; two only through an Awakener's Orb (15 combinations, the wiki). Since the influenced Exalted Orbs refuse every influenced item, they can never make a second influence: the limit is a state invariant that only #90's Awakener can reach.
- **Orb of Dominance.** On a body armour, boots, gloves or helmet (magic or rare) with at least two influenced mods: removes one influenced mod and upgrades another one tier; a top-tier mod becomes its **Elevated** version (247 mods in the export, spawn weight 0, only reachable this way); an Elevated mod is rerolled in value. Craft of Exile's code: both picks uniform, the removed first among the eligible mods, the upgraded second among the rest, so each ordered pair has chance 1/(n(n-1)). `Prefixes/Suffixes Cannot Be Changed` protect a side from both picks; Attack/Caster metamods are ignored.
- **Harvest.** Two influence crafts: Randomise the influence types of an influenced normal, magic or rare item and reforge it (1,000 Primal); Reforge an influenced rare with random mods including an influenced mod (5,000 Primal + 1 Sacred). The second is Craft of Exile's tagged reforge with the tag "Influence" (a mod of an influence group of the item). There is no "reforge keeping an influenced mod" craft in the wiki list: the ticket's wording is this guaranteed-influence reforge.
- **Beast recipes.** Six, one per influence, each a named beast plus a Craicic Maw; the wiki says recipes that add mods use the item's item level. The wiki and the export do not say which mod is added; the natural reading (an influence mod of that item) is unverified.
- **Metamods.** Exalted Orb and "Conqueror variants": locks respected (open slots on the locked side may still be filled), Attack/Caster respected; Dominance: locks respected, Attack/Caster ignored; Awakener: ignores all four; Randomise Influence: locks respected but usage prevented while the locked side holds an influenced mod. Crafted mods and metamods occupy slots as in #11.

**INF-09** The pool of an influence on an item is: `domain` item prefix and suffix mods that have a positive weight on `<class key>_<influence key>` (first matching spawn tag as for any draw), `required_level` at most the item level, family not on the item, side not full; weight = that spawn weight. **INF-11** The orbs respect the locks and the type blocks: `Prefixes/Suffixes Cannot Be Changed` respected but open slots on the locked side may be filled; `Cannot roll Attack/Caster Modifiers` respected. ### Orb of Dominance and Elevated mods

**INF-13** Gate: a magic or rare body armour, boots, gloves or helmet with at least two influenced mods. Kind: wiki. URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Orb_of_Dominance. Quote: "It can be used on Magic and Rare Body Armours, Boots, Gloves and Helmets with at least two influence modifiers." (item text: "Can be used on Body Armours, Boots, Gloves and Helmets"). CoE's gate is weaker or different: `"required":{"ilvl":68,"influenced":!0,"flags":["is_influenced","is_rare"]},"cannot":{"rarity":"normal"}` (rare flag, no class list, but CoE has Elevated data only for the four classes, INF-17). O-04. Patch 3.29.

**INF-14** One influenced mod is removed and another upgraded; the orb does not distinguish influence types. Kind: wiki. Quote: "It randomly upgrades one of these influenced modifiers and removes one other influenced modifier." and "If your items have multiple influence types, the orb doesn't distinguish between different influence types in any way, and may pick any mod at random." Patch 3.29.

**INF-15** Odds: the removed mod is uniform among the eligible mods, then the upgraded mod is uniform among the remaining eligible ones, so a specific (removed, upgraded) pair has chance 1/(n(n-1)) and a given mod is upgraded with chance 1/n. Kind: coe. Quote: `if(nelig.length>=2){var rndpick=poec_rand(0,nelig.length,!0);var rchc=1/nelig.length;var rem=nelig.splice(rndpick,1);...var uchc=1/nelig.length;var aug=nelig[rndpick]`. Eligible = an explicit mod in an influence mod group (`crsim_infgrps`) whose side is not protected by a `Cannot Be Changed` lock. The wiki says "randomly" without weights; no sample was found (O-05). Patch 3.29.0.3.

**INF-16** Upgrade: a tier 3 mod becomes tier 2 (the next better tier of the same mod); a top-tier mod becomes its Elevated version; an Elevated mod is rerolled in value. No item level is needed for the Elevated step. Kind: wiki + coe. URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Orb_of_Dominance. Quotes: "A tier 3 mod will be replaced with its tier 2 version, for example."; "There is no item level requirement for a mod to become an elevated tier."; item text "Attempting to upgrade an Elevated Modifier rerolls its values". CoE: `if(useaffixes[aug].tindex+1==ntiers||useaffixes[aug].tindex=="e"){...tindex="e"...rolls=poec_simRollValues(...)}else{var utier=poecd.tiers[...][tindex+1]`. Whether a non-Elevated upgrade checks the next tier's item level is not stated (CoE does not; O-06). Patch 3.29.

**INF-17** Locks: `Prefixes/Suffixes Cannot Be Changed` respected (a mod on the locked side is neither removed nor upgraded); `Cannot roll Attack/Caster Modifiers` ignored. Kind: wiki + coe. URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Metamod#Rules_and_Exceptions (row "Orb of Dominance": Respected | Respected | Ignored | Ignored); also https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Orb_of_Dominance: "modifiers that can't be changed (e.g. suffixes on an item with the "Suffixes can't be changed" mod) will not be affected." CoE: `if(atype=="prefix"){if(usetmetas.nchg_pre){metaprotect=!0}}...if(!metaprotect){nelig.push(i)}`. With a lock on one side only the other side's influenced mods are eligible, and at least two are needed. Patch 3.29.

**INF-18** Elevated mods: 247 records in the export, names "Elevated Shaper's", "of the Elevated Elder" and so on (Shaper 48, Elder 47, Redeemer 39, Hunter 39, Crusader 37, Warlord 37), every spawn weight 0, the same `groups` (family) as their ordinary tiers; they exist for helmet 67, gloves 65, body armour 60, boots 58 and a few more tags of other classes that a Dominance cannot reach (amulet 14, quiver 13, weapons, belt 1, ring 1). Only the Orb of Dominance makes them. Kind: datamined + wiki. URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Elevated_modifier. Quote: "These modifiers can only be crafted onto Rare Influenced items using a Orb of Dominance". Patch 3.29.3.3.

**INF-19** Which Elevated mod a top-tier mod becomes. CoE holds an explicit table `poecd.maeven.bmods["<base id>-<mod id>"]` (1,661 entries, only helmet, gloves, boots and body armour bases) and reads it in the Dominance code. The export has no link field; by name stem ("...Uber3" to "...UberMaven") 216 of the 247 map cleanly to a top tier that has weight, 26 map to a mod whose top tier has weight 0 (disabled), 5 have no stem match (`AdditionalCurseOnEnemiesInfluenceMavenV2___`, `RegenerateLifeOverMaven`, three `Maximum...ResistanceInfluenceMavenNew`). Use CoE's table as the mapping and the stem as a check (O-07). Kind: coe + datamined. Patch 3.29.0.3 / 3.29.3.3.

### Harvest

**INF-23** The guaranteed-influence reforge is the tagged reforge with the tag "Influence": in Craft of Exile's code the tag `inf` selects the mods whose mod group is an influence group of the item (`if(tagName=="inf"){if(crsim_infgrps.indexOf("|"+mods[j].id_mgroup+"|")>-1){hasATag=!0}}`), and the type list of the Harvest chooser has "Influence" and "Non-Influence" entries. **INF-24** Tagged Harvest reforges respect the Attack/Caster blocks since 3.27.0. Kind: wiki (official notes). URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Version_3.27.0. ### Interactions

**INF-28** Crafted mods and metamods: a bench-crafted mod, a metamod and a mod from any other source each occupy an affix slot; the influenced Exalted Orb needs an open slot, so a full six-mod item is refused (BEN notes #11). Dominance does not touch crafted mods (they are not influenced mods); a metamod on the item protects a whole side (INF-17). The Awakener ignores all metamods (wiki, `Metamod` table). Kind: wiki + coe. Patch 3.29.

**INF-29** Quality: catalyst quality does not interact with any of these orbs after 3.15.0 (INF-10); the Hunter, Crusader and other orbs keep quality. Kind: wiki. Patch 3.15.0.

## Benchmark checks

- **R02** (Archdemon Crown, Shaper, item level 100): step 1 is a Shaper's Exalted Orb on a start rare without influence. Allowed by INF-04 to INF-07 if the start item has no influence and an open slot; the helmet Shaper pool is 11 prefix records / 20 suffix records at item level 100 (INF-09). ## Open points

- **O-01 Same-influence Exalted Orb.** Three specific wiki pages, the item help text and CoE refuse every influenced item; the wiki's `Influenced item` page has one broad sentence ("different than the orb") that can be read the other way. Settle by a one-minute in-game check (a Hunter item and a Hunter's Exalted Orb, hover the orb) or the owner's knowledge. Recommended answer: refused.
- **O-02 Eldritch implicit.** The Shaper page's lead says an item with an Eldritch implicit counts as non-influenced and can take the orb; its Mechanics section, the Hunter page, the `Modifier` page and CoE refuse it. Recommended answer: refused (the item cannot be given an influence, INF-07).
- **O-03 Shaper's and Elder's metamod behaviour.** The Metamod table names "Exalted Orb and Conqueror variants". Craft of Exile treats all six alike. Recommended: alike.
- **O-04 Dominance class and rarity gate.** Wiki: body armour, boots, gloves, helmets, magic and rare. CoE: rare only, no class list. Recommended: wiki (the Elevated data in CoE is those four classes too). A magic item with two influenced mods has no use in a rare target route.
- **O-05 Dominance odds.** Uniform picks come from CoE's code, the wiki says "randomly" only; no sample read. Recommended: use 1/(n(n-1)); mark claim as `coe`.
- **O-06 Dominance upgrade and item level.** Does the next tier below the top need the item level (a tier-2 mod needs level 75, the item is level 70)? CoE ignores it; the wiki says only that the Elevated step has no item level. Recommended: ignore the item level.
- **O-07 Elevated mapping.** Five Elevated mods cannot be linked by name stem; CoE's `maeven.bmods` table links them by id. A coder needs a data task to convert CoE's table (ids differ from the export's mod ids: CoE ids are numeric, the export's are strings; match by text and tier values).
- **O-08 Influence reforge and Attack/Caster blocks.** Wiki table: "Unknown" for the Randomise craft; 3.27.0 says tagged reforges respect them; CoE says ignored. Same as the open point O-03 of #11. Recommended: respected, as for the Lightning reforge.
- **O-09 Beast recipes.** The mod pool, whether the item must carry that influence (the recipe name says so), whether an open slot is needed and whether the metamods are respected are not in the wiki or the export. Recommended model: an add as the influenced Exalted Orb of that influence, on an item that already has the influence, with the item's own level, respecting locks and type blocks; unverified.
- **O-10 Harvest Randomise.** Not modelled by CoE. After the craft: how many influences (one or two), which, with which chances, and what happens to a dual-influenced item are not stated anywhere read. Recommended: leave out of v1 unless a route needs it (none of R02, R06, R20 does; R20 uses the reforge). Ask the owner whether to measure it. *#112:* the only player use found: [u/PlayForFund, r/pathofexile, 2024-10-23, thread "need advice on how to craft" 1gagnt4](https://www.reddit.com/r/pathofexile/comments/1gagnt4/), score 19, a VLS-dagger route on a banishing blade: "5.
