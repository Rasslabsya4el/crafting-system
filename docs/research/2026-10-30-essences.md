# Essences in 3.29 (research ticket #8)

Ticket: [#8 Essences in 3.29](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/8), map [#1](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/1), evidence rule [#2 Q2](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/2).
Date checked: 2026-10-30. Patch: 3.29.3.3 (ordinary, non-Ruthless). Claim-id prefix `ESS`.

**Answer.** An essence is a currency that, on a normal item, makes a rare with one guaranteed ("forced") affix plus random affixes drawn Alchemy-style, and (tiers 5-7 and the four corrupted essences only) on a rare item rerolls it Chaos-style with the same guaranteed affix. The forced affix is not random: `essences.json` maps each essence record and each of 22 equipment item classes to one exact `mods.json` mod id (a specific tier of an ordinary family in 285 cases, an `is_essence_only` mod in 337 cases). Tiers 1-4 (Whispering..Wailing) accept normal items only and restrict the *random* affixes to mod level <= 35/45/60/75 (or the item level if lower). Tiers 5-7 and corrupted accept normal or rare items, with no cap. Essences are refused on corrupted items and (wiki; the 2016 official post says "ignore", O-09) on items carrying an outcome-affecting metamod (`Prefixes/Suffixes Cannot Be Changed`, `Cannot roll Attack/Caster Modifiers`); `Can have up to 3 Crafted Modifiers` alone does not block. A fractured mod is permanent. Obtainable in 3.29: the 20 natural families x tiers by drop/vendor/Vaal, the four corrupted essences only by Vaal Orb on a group-E monolith; Essence of Desolation is drop-disabled and Remnant of Corruption is gone. **Not settled by any admitted source (blocks coding):** how many random affixes are drawn and whether the forced affix counts inside the 4-6 total (O-01), what happens when the forced mod shares a family with a retained fractured mod (O-03), whether the forced mod ignores a base item level below its mod level (O-02), and whether Rune Dagger / Warstaff accept essences (O-05).

Source kinds: `official` = pathofexile.com forum / patch notes; `wiki` = poewiki.net; `datamined` = repoe-fork 3.29.3.3 export (`https://repoe-fork.github.io/<file>`).

## 1. How `essences.json` encodes the forced mod (read this before the claims)

`essences.json` is an object of 106 records keyed by the essence item's metadata id (e.g. `Metadata/Items/Currency/CurrencyEssenceRage4`). Record fields (all 106 records have exactly these):

| field | meaning (from observed values) |
|---|---|
| `name` | item name, e.g. `Shrieking Essence of Rage` |
| `level` | 1-7 = Whispering..Deafening; 8 = the four corrupted essences; 0 = `Remnant of Corruption` and `Essence of Desolation` |
| `type.tier` | family group: 1 = group A (Hatred, Woe, Greed, Contempt), 2 = B (Sorrow, Anger, Torment, Fear), 3 = C (Suffering, Rage, Wrath, Doubt), 4 = D (Anguish, Loathing, Spite, Zeal), 5 = E (Misery, Dread, Scorn, Envy), 6 = corrupted four and Desolation, 0 = Remnant |
| `type.is_corruption_only` | true for Hysteria, Insanity, Horror, Delirium |
| `item_level_restriction` | cap on the level of the *random* mods: 35 / 45 / 60 / 75 for `level` 1 / 2 / 3 / 4; `null` for `level` >= 5 and corrupted; 15 for Desolation |
| `spawn_level_min` | minimum monster level for the drop (1, 12, 30, 48, 68, 73 for levels 1-6; 0 for level 7 and corrupted); a drop fact, not a crafting rule |
| `mods` | object: item-class name -> mod id (string) or `null` |

**Mapping an essence + a base item to a mod id.** (1) Take the base's `item_class` from `base_items.json` (`https://repoe-fork.github.io/base_items.json`, field `item_class`, e.g. `Body Armour`, `One Hand Sword`, `Bow`). (2) Look it up in `essences[essence_id].mods`. (3) The value is a key of `mods.json`; that record gives `generation_type` (prefix/suffix), `required_level`, `groups` (always exactly one family), `type`, `adds_tags`, `generation_weights`, `stats`, `text`, `is_essence_only`. (4) A class absent from the 22 keys has no mapping (Rune Dagger, Warstaff, Jewel, flasks...). (5) The `Remnant of Corruption` record has `null` for every class and must be skipped. The 22 class keys match `base_items.json` `item_class` strings exactly.

**The 22 classes collapse to 13 behaviour clusters** (identical mod id for every one of the 106 records inside a cluster): `Amulet`; `Belt`; `Body Armour`; `Boots`; `Bow`; `Gloves`; `Helmet`; `Quiver`; `Ring`; `Shield`; `Wand`; the one-hand melee/sceptre group {`Claw`, `Dagger`, `One Hand Axe`, `One Hand Mace`, `One Hand Sword`, `Sceptre`, `Thrusting One Hand Sword`}; the two-hand group {`Staff`, `Two Hand Axe`, `Two Hand Mace`, `Two Hand Sword`}. (Bow, Wand, one-hand group and two-hand group differ from each other for 83 of 106 records.)

**Integrity checks run on 3.29.3.3** (scripts in `.claude/scratch/research-essences/`, not committed): 2310 non-null mappings, 622 distinct mod ids, all 622 exist in `mods.json`, all have `domain = "item"`, one group each; 350 prefixes and 272 suffixes among the distinct ids; 285 distinct ids are ordinary mods (`is_essence_only = false`), 337 are `is_essence_only = true`.

## 2. Admitted claims

Each: rule, source kind, URL, quote, patch the source speaks for, checked 2026-10-30. "Patch" for a wiki page = the newest entry of that page's Version history table (read 2026-10-30), i.e. nothing newer changed the rule.

**ESS-01 - Lookup of the forced mod.** The forced mod of an essence on a given base is `mods[item_class]` of the essence's record in `essences.json`, resolved in `mods.json`; one exact mod id per (essence, class); no randomness in the choice; classes outside the 22 listed have no mapping; the `Remnant of Corruption` record maps to `null` everywhere.
- datamined, `https://repoe-fork.github.io/essences.json`, record `Metadata/Items/Currency/CurrencyEssenceRage4`: `"mods": {"Helmet": "Strength8", ...}`; record `Metadata/Items/Currency/CurrencyCorruptMonolith`: every class `null`. Patch 3.29.3.3.

**ESS-02 - Shape of the forced mod.** Every forced mod is a `mods.json` record with `domain: "item"`, exactly one entry in `groups` (its family for exclusion), `generation_type` prefix or suffix (that decides the slot it takes), its own `required_level`, `adds_tags` (163 of the 622 forced ids carry `has_attack_mod` 103 / `has_caster_mod` 60) and `generation_weights` (56 ids). The same essence forces a prefix on some classes and a suffix on others (e.g. Hatred: weapons prefix, armour suffix).
- datamined, `https://repoe-fork.github.io/mods.json`, ids `Strength8` (`generation_type: suffix`, `required_level: 74`, `groups: ["Strength"]`), `LocalAddedColdDamageEssence7` (`prefix`, 82, `groups: ["ColdDamage"]`, `adds_tags: ["has_attack_mod"]`). Patch 3.29.3.3.

**ESS-03 - Essence levels and families.** There are 100 natural essence records (20 families, `level` 1-7, availability per family in section 1), four corrupted records (`level` 8, `is_corruption_only: true`: Hysteria, Insanity, Horror, Delirium), `Essence of Desolation` (`level` 0) and `Remnant of Corruption` (`level` 0, not an essence craft).
- datamined, `https://repoe-fork.github.io/essences.json`, e.g. `CurrencyEssenceHorror1`: `"level": 8, "type": {"is_corruption_only": true, "tier": 6}`; `CurrencyEssenceWoe3`: `"level": 3, "type": {"is_corruption_only": false, "tier": 1}`. Patch 3.29.3.3. Wiki names the tiers: [Essence § Crafting](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Essence#Crafting) table, `1 Whispering ... 7 Deafening`. Patch 3.29.3.

**ESS-04 - Cap on the random mods of tiers 1-4.** For essences of tier 1-4 the *random* affixes are drawn only from mods whose level is at most the cap; if the item level is lower than the cap the item level binds, so the effective level is `min(item level, cap)`. Cap = 35 (Whispering), 45 (Muttering), 60 (Weeping), 75 (Wailing). Tiers 5-7 and corrupted have no cap. The forced mod is not subject to the cap (the cap is stated for "random modifiers").
- wiki, [Essence § Crafting](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Essence#Crafting): "Low tier essences restrict the pool of random modifiers that can roll when using the essence up to a certain maximum level, if the maximum modifier level is lower than the item's actual item level." Table column "Maximum level for random mods": 35/45/60/75/n/a/n/a/n/a. Patch 3.29.3.
- datamined (numbers), `essences.json` field `item_level_restriction`: `CurrencyEssenceHatred1..4` = 35, 45, 60, 75; `CurrencyEssenceHatred5..7` = `null`. Patch 3.29.3.3.

**ESS-05 - Valid input rarities.** Tiers 1-4 (Whispering..Wailing): normal items only. Tiers 5-7 (Screaming, Shrieking, Deafening) and the four corrupted essences: normal or rare items. Magic is accepted by no tier. (Tier 5 became rare-capable in 3.14.0; before that only tiers 6-7.)
- datamined, `https://repoe-fork.github.io/base_items.json`, `Metadata/Items/Currency/CurrencyEssenceHatred1` (and the other 39 records of levels 1-4): `properties.description` "Upgrades a normal item to rare with one guaranteed property", `properties.directions` "Right click this item then left click a normal item to apply it."; `CurrencyEssenceRage4` and the other 64 records (levels 5-8 and Essence of Desolation, incl. Horror): "Upgrades a normal item to rare or reforges a rare item, guaranteeing one property" / "Right click this item then left click a normal or rare item to apply it." Patch 3.29.3.3.
- official, [All Rebalanced Essences](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3080281), Community_Team, 2021-04-15 (3.14.0), entry Screaming Essence of Hatred: Added "Upgrades a normal item to rare or reforges a rare item, guaranteeing one property"; Removed "Upgrades a normal item to rare with one guaranteed property". Same for all 20 Screaming essences.
- wiki, [Essence § Crafting](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Essence#Crafting), column "Item reforge": No for tiers 1-4, Yes for 5-7; text "Essences of tier 5 (Screaming) and above can also be used to reforge the affixes of rare equipment, like a Chaos Orb, with a guaranteed affix." Patch 3.29.3 (3.14.0 history line: "Screaming Essences can now be used to reforge rare items").

**ESS-06 - Essence on a normal item.** Produces a rare item with one guaranteed affix (the forced mod) plus random affixes; the random part works like an Orb of Alchemy (subject to ESS-04). Official statement of the same design: essences on normal items "work like Orbs of Alchemy".
- wiki, [Essence § Crafting](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Essence#Crafting): "All essences can be used to upgrade a piece of normal rarity equipment to rare, similar to an Orb of Alchemy, and apply one guaranteed affix." Patch 3.29.3.
- official, [Development Manifesto - Essence Metamod Crafting](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/1713551), Chris, 2016-08-22 (2.4.0): "Currently the top two power levels of each type also work like Chaos Orbs" (implying the rest work as Alchemy: the same post names "Have all essences only work like Orbs of Alchemies" as the alternative of making every tier Alchemy-only).

**ESS-07 - Essence on a rare item (tiers 5-7, corrupted).** Rerolls the item's non-permanent affixes like a Chaos Orb and guarantees the forced mod. Valid only for the tiers of ESS-05.
- wiki, [Essence § Crafting](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Essence#Crafting): "Essences of tier 5 (Screaming) and above can also be used to reforge the affixes of rare equipment, like a Chaos Orb, with a guaranteed affix." Patch 3.29.3. Corrupted: [Essence § Corrupted essences](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Essence#Corrupted_essences) Reforge column shows both the Alchemy icon ("Reforges normal items") and the Chaos icon ("Reforges rare items"); datamined directions in ESS-05 confirm it.

**ESS-08 - Count rule of Chaos (restated, context for O-01).** A Chaos Orb on a regular item gives a rare with 4, 5 or 6 affixes at 8:3:1. (Already accepted in ticket #2; restated with its source. Whether an essence reuses this count is open, O-01.)
- wiki, [Chaos Orb § Mechanics](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Chaos_Orb#Mechanics): "For regular items, Chaos Orbs result in a rare item with an affix count of 4, 5, or 6 at a ratio of 8:3:1." Patch 3.28.0e (newest history entry).

**ESS-09 - Count rule of Alchemy (restated, context for O-01).** An Orb of Alchemy gives a rare with 4 to 6 affixes (3 to 4 on jewels); the page gives no ratio.
- wiki, [Orb of Alchemy § Spending](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Orb_of_Alchemy#Spending): "The resulting rare has randomly chosen affixes and has a random number of affixes (ranging from four to six (three to four if used on jewels))." Patch 3.28.0.

**ESS-10 - Four metamods block essences.** An essence cannot be used on an item that has `Prefixes Cannot Be Changed`, `Suffixes Cannot Be Changed`, `Cannot roll Attack Modifiers` or `Cannot roll Caster Modifiers`; the attempt is refused with `Failed to apply item: You can't use this currency on items with a modifier that affects mod outcomes` and nothing changes. Craft order consequence: essence first, metamod after.
- wiki, [Metamod § Rules and Exceptions](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Meta-crafting_mod#Rules_and_Exceptions), row Essences: "Usage Prevented" under all four metamod columns; [§ Essence and Fossil](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Meta-crafting_mod#Essence_and_Fossil): "the game will warn you that Failed to apply item: You can't use this currency on items with a modifier that affects mod outcomes." Also [Essence § Crafting](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Essence#Crafting): "Essences cannot be used on an item if it has a meta-crafting mod". Patch: Metamod page newest entry 3.27.0 (about Harvest, not essences); Essence page 3.29.3. NOTE the 2016 official wording differs: see O-09.

**ESS-11 - `Can have up to 3 Crafted Modifiers` does not block essences.** The multi-craft metamod is the one exception to ESS-10.
- wiki, [Metamod § Essence and Fossil](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Meta-crafting_mod#Essence_and_Fossil): "When using essence or fossil on an item with metamod (other then the multi-craft mod), the game will warn you". Patch 3.27.0 (newest entry). The general sentence on the Essence page ("a meta-crafting mod") is superseded here by the specific exception.

**ESS-12 - Corrupted items refuse essences.** Corrupted items cannot be modified; the page's exception list contains no essence.
- wiki, [Corrupted](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Corrupted): "Corrupted items cannot be modified, with the following exceptions:" (exceptions: crafting-bench sockets, Tainted currency, Tainted Catalyst/Oil, certain uniques, Divine Font, Djinn-Touched Vaal Orb). Patch 3.29.0 (newest entry).

**ESS-13 - A fractured mod is permanent.** Fractured mods cannot be removed or have their values changed by crafting (Annulment and Scouring named, "etc."), so an essence reforge of a fractured rare cannot remove the fractured mod. Essences are not named on that page; the consequence for essences rests on "permanent" and is the basis of O-03.
- wiki, [Fractured item § Mechanics](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Fractured_item#Mechanics): "Fractured items have at least one permanent "Fractured" mod." and "These mods cannot be removed (by Orb of Annulment or Orb of Scouring, etc.) or have its values changed by Divine Orb." Patch 3.20.0 (newest entry).

**ESS-14 - `is_essence_only` mods.** 462 `mods.json` records are flagged `is_essence_only: true`; all 462 have an all-zero `spawn_weights` (so no ordinary pool ever draws them: they exist only as forced essence mods or on legacy/drop items). 337 of the 622 forced ids are such mods; 285 forced ids are ordinary mods (the forced item is a specific tier of a normal family, e.g. `Strength8`, `ColdResist8`). 125 flagged mods are referenced by no essence record (legacy variants, e.g. `IncreasedLifeEssenceChest1`; unreachable by a 3.29 essence craft). The flag is not a complete "essence-only" test: `LightningDamagePercent6`, forced by Shrieking Essence of Wrath on Ring and Amulet, is not flagged but has weight 0 for wand, sceptre and default.
- datamined, `https://repoe-fork.github.io/mods.json`, ids `ColdDamagePercentEssence1` (`is_essence_only: true`, `spawn_weights: [{"tag": "default", "weight": 0}]`), `ColdResist1` (`is_essence_only: false`, positive weights on armour, ring, amulet, belt, quiver), `LightningDamagePercent6`. Patch 3.29.3.3.

**ESS-15 - Natural essence sources and tier ceilings.** Natural essences (groups A-E, tiers 1-6) drop from monsters trapped in essence monoliths; Essence Monoliths in White Maps spawn up to Screaming, in Yellow or Red Maps up to Shrieking. Other sources: Harvest craft (changes a stack of essences into another type of the same tier), Tujen/Ritual shops, divination cards (e.g. Harmony of Souls 9x Shrieking, The Cacophony 3x Deafening), league rewards.
- wiki, [Essence § Obtaining essences](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Essence#Obtaining_essences): "Essence Monoliths in White Maps can spawn up to a maximum tier of Screaming, while those in Yellow or Red Maps can spawn up to a maximum tier of Shrieking." Patch 3.24.0 (history line), page 3.29.3.

**ESS-16 - Deafening comes only from upgrade or Vaal.** Tier 7 is obtained by the 3-to-1 upgrade (3 essences of the same type and tier -> 1 of the same type one tier higher, vendor recipe or Essence stash tab) or by a Vaal Orb on a monolith holding Shrieking essences.
- wiki, [Essence § Vendor recipes](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Essence#Vendor_recipes): "1x essence of the same type one tier higher / 3x essence of the same type and tier"; [§ Obtaining](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Essence#Obtaining_essences): "Tier 7, "Deafening" essences can be obtained by upgrading lower tier essences through the vendor recipe system or by corrupting an essence monolith with tier 6 (Shrieking) essences using a Vaal Orb." Patch 3.29.3.

**ESS-17 - Corrupted essences in 3.29.** Essence of Hysteria / Insanity / Horror / Delirium are obtainable only by using a Vaal Orb on an essence monolith that holds a group-E essence (Scorn, Envy, Misery, Dread; any tier). Remnants of Corruption are no longer obtainable (3.27.0); the `Remnant of Corruption` record in `essences.json` is a leftover with `null` mods.
- official, [Patch Notes - Content Update 3.27.0](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3869068), Stacey_GGG, 2025-10-24: "Vaal Orbs can now be used on monsters trapped in Essences, and will function as Remnants of Corruption did previously." and "Remnants of Corruption will no longer be obtainable, and existing Remnants of Corruption will be converted to Vaal Orbs." Patch 3.27.0.
- wiki, [Essence § Corrupting essence monsters](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Essence#Corrupting_essence_monsters): "Essence of Hysteria, Insanity, Horror and Delirium can only be obtained by corrupting monsters with an essence of group E". Patch 3.29.3.

**ESS-18 - Essence of Desolation is not obtainable.** Drop-disabled since 3.28.0l (Mirage content was not added to the core game); the 3.29.3 fix stopped the Atlas-passive rare-item path from dropping items with its modifiers. Its record in `essences.json` exists (`level` 0, `item_level_restriction` 15) but there is no ordinary 3.29 source.

## 3. Open (not admitted)

Blockers for coding the essence primitive are marked **[BLOCKS]**.

**O-01 [BLOCKS] How many random affixes, and does the forced affix count inside the total?** Known: essence-on-normal "similar to an Orb of Alchemy" (ESS-06; Alchemy 4-6, no ratio, ESS-09); essence-on-rare "like a Chaos Orb" (ESS-07; Chaos 4/5/6 at 8:3:1, ESS-08). Not stated by any admitted source: whether the 4-6 total includes the forced mod, whether essence-on-normal uses 8:3:1 or another ratio, whether essence-on-rare keeps retained (fractured) mods inside the total. Non-admitted hints (cross-check only): a 2016 forum reply "It's just an alchemy or chaos orb with one guaranteed mod" (view-thread/1790647); a 2022 forum post "3 added mods (4 with the fractured mod) roughly 90% of the time" on fractured items, no sample size (view-thread/3300578); a Reddit experiment of 1000 Chaos rerolls on one base, 674/239/87 = 4/5/6 (r/pathofexile ex2c5o, 2020, Chaos only). Settles it: a large-sample experiment (e.g. >= 2000 Screaming..Deafening essences on ilvl >= 84 normal bases and as many on non-fractured rares, tallying total explicit counts, with a Chaos control), or a GGG statement.

**O-02 [BLOCKS for item level < forced mod level] Does the forced mod ignore the item level?** The forced mod's `required_level` reaches 86 (level-7 records; e.g. `ColdResist8` 84, `Strength8` 74); the wiki says item level restricts "random properties" and the essence page column in ESS-19 implies the item gets that required level, but no admitted source says an essence applies on a base whose item level is below the forced mod's level. Hint (not admitted): a 2019 forum report of an ilvl 23 staff crafted with Shrieking/Deafening Essence of Rage, result cut off (view-thread/2329214). Settles it: a wiki/GGG sentence, or an in-game test with the applied item shown.

**O-03 [BLOCKS fractured routes R05, R10, R11, R22-R24] Essence on a rare with fractured mods.** ESS-13 says a fractured mod is permanent, but no admitted source states (a) that the essence reforge keeps it and counts it inside the 4-6 total and the 3+3 slot limits, (b) what happens when the forced mod's family equals a retained fractured mod's family (error and no change? roll anyway?), (c) whether two essence-flagged mods may coexist. Hints (not admitted): player answers that "The fractured stat cant be removed"; Craft of Exile code rejects a conflicting fractured group (prototype note); a PoE 2 error "This item already has a mod of this type" is not transferable. Settles it: GGG staff statement or an in-game test (screenshot of the error) of Essence of X on an item fractured with the same family.

**O-04 Do bench-crafted (non-meta) mods survive an essence reforge?** Not stated for essences; Chaos Orb page says it rerolls "all modifiers" (wiki). Only relevant for crafted mods other than metamods (metamods block essences, ESS-10). Settles it: wiki sentence or test.

**O-05 [BLOCKS classes Rune Dagger, Warstaff] Which item classes accept essences.** `essences.json` has no mapping for `Rune Dagger` (18 bases), `Warstaff` (12 bases), `Jewel`, `AbyssJewel`, flasks, `Tincture`, `FishingRod` (ESS-01). No admitted source states whether the game refuses or applies another mapping on those. Hint (not admitted): player posts craft Warstaves/daggers with essences, ambiguous about the class. Settles it: wiki sentence, or a test on a Rune Dagger and a Warstaff.

**O-06 Influenced / Synthesised / Mirrored / Split inputs.** No admitted source on essences with these item states (the Influenced, Synthesised, Mirrored pages say nothing about essences). Matters from B2 (influence). Settles it: wiki or test.

**O-07 Legacy metamod `of Dueling` (Cannot roll Modifiers with Required Level above 28).** The Metamod page says it exists only on old items; the interaction with essences is not stated (ESS-10 covers four metamods). Low priority.

**O-08 Essence of Desolation scope.** Existing stock may still be traded or held; the record has `item_level_restriction` 15 (poedb, cross-check, shows "Properties restricted to level 15 and below"). Decision for the main chat: include as an action or exclude as unobtainable (ESS-18). Not a research question.

**O-09 Wiki says "Usage Prevented", the 2016 official post says "ignore".** [Development Manifesto - Essence Metamod Crafting](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/1713551), Chris, 2016-08-22: "Our solution is that Essences will ignore master metamods when rolling an item." The wiki cites this post for the opposite reading (an error and no change, ESS-10). A 2017 player report in the forum (view-thread/1868570) says the essence rerolled the item and removed "Prefixes Cannot Be Changed". Under the evidence rule the wiki (current behaviour) wins over an older official statement, so ESS-10 is admitted; the risk is that 3.29 still ignores the metamod. Settles it: a later GGG statement or a 3.29 in-game test of an essence on a "Prefixes Cannot Be Changed" item, showing either the error text or the rerolled item.

**O-10 How the forced mod enters the later draw.** The forced mod carries `adds_tags` (`has_attack_mod` / `has_caster_mod`) and `generation_weights` (ESS-02); how those weight the remaining draws and whether the family of the forced mod is excluded from the random pool is the generic affix-draw question (ticket on affix draws; family exclusion is already accepted in #2). Not essence-specific.

**O-11 Do the four corrupted essences have any special mapping rule beyond ESS-05?** The prototype said "tier 8 remains separately gated by its special mapping rules"; the data shows the same record shape and input rules as tiers 5-7. No special rule found in any source. Settles it: nothing needed unless a source names one.
