# Brief: the crafting bench "Reroll" options in Path of Exile 1 (3.28–3.29)

Report file: `docs/research/2026-10-30-bench-rerolls.md`

Context: since about patch 3.28 the crafting bench offers, for rare items, "Reroll 3 random Modifiers on a Rare Item" for 3 Chaos Orbs and an option that rerolls 1 random modifier for 8 Chaos Orbs. Find how players and GGG describe them.

Questions ("not found" is an allowed answer; every answer needs a Reddit, forum or patch-note URL with a short verbatim quote):
1. The exact in-game names, costs and patch of introduction of these bench options, from GGG patch notes or forum posts.
2. Which modifiers they can pick: any explicit modifier, or not crafted, fractured, or ones protected by "Prefixes Cannot Be Changed" / "Suffixes Cannot Be Changed"? Is the pick uniform?
3. What replaces the removed modifiers: a new random modifier of the same side (prefix for prefix), any side, a fixed count? Does the item's number of modifiers stay the same? Can the same modifier come back?
4. How they interact with "Cannot roll Attack Modifiers" / "Cannot roll Caster Modifiers", Memory Strands, influence and Eldritch implicits. Do players say they behave like Harvest reforges in these interactions?
5. Any experiments or sample counts players report, and any GGG staff reply.
