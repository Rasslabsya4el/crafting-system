# The essence plans the `actions:` key hides on R08, R09 and R13 (#627)

Ticket [#627](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/627), open point of #475 (PR #614). Checked 2026-11-12 on main `fc77615`, game data repoe-fork 3.29.3.3, Craft of Exile copy of `.claude/scratch/coe/site` (data 3.29.0.3). Rules: OR-2, OR-5, OR-8, OR-13, OR-16, OR-21, OR-24, OR-26.

## The question

With the `actions:` key removed from a copy of the target (`.github/scripts/actions_key.py`, `removed`, run 42369553590), R08 and R09 plan with one essence rerolled again and again (Shrieking Essence of Fear, Screaming Essence of Spite) for 74% less than the fossil plan, and R13 adds Deafening Essence of Hatred for 6% less. Is that a defect of the essence model, of the forced mods, or of the prices?

## Verdict

**The mechanics are right; the size of the R08 and R09 saving is a price question, and that question is #693.** Nothing in the essence model or the forced mods is wrong, so no code of the planner changes here.

### 1. The forced mods are the game's (OR-2, OR-21)

From `essences.json` of 3.29.3.3 (claim `essence.forced_mod_by_item_class`), and the same in the emulator on every item (`tools/coe_emulator/compare.py`, 400 uses each, 2026-11-12):

| route | essence | item | forced mod | goal line it meets |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R08 | Shrieking Essence of Fear (level 6) | Sorcerer Boots, ilvl 86, rare | `MinionLifeEssence6`, suffix, Minions have (25-27)% increased maximum Life | none: a filler suffix |
| R09 | Screaming Essence of Spite (level 5) | Two-Toned Boots (Evasion/ES), ilvl 86, rare | `Intelligence6`, suffix, +(33-37) to Intelligence | none: a filler suffix |
| R13 | Deafening Essence of Hatred (level 7) | Leather Belt, ilvl 86, rare | `ColdResist8`, suffix, +(46-48)% to Cold Resistance | the tier 1 of the Cold Resistance line |

The emulator applied all three (none refused), and put the forced mod on 400 of 400 items; no candidate (no |z| > 4) in the mods or the shapes of the rest.

### 2. The reroll is a Chaos Orb with one fixed suffix (claim `essence.rare_item_reforged_like_chaos`)

A Screaming or higher essence on a rare item rerolls it like a Chaos Orb and keeps the forced mod (wiki, Essence, "Crafting"). On R08 and R09 the forced mod is a suffix no line asks, and Movement Speed is a suffix too, so the essence is a Chaos Orb that spends one suffix slot. The planner's chance that one use ends in the target or one bench craft short of it (`cli/_benchmark_use.per_use_of` on the target with the key removed):

| route | essence, one use | Chaos Orb, one use |
|---|---|---|
| R08 | 2.23% (0.089% the target, 2.14% one bench craft short) | 1.94% |
| R09 | 2.36% (0.104% + 2.26%) | 2.13% |

So per use the essence is as good as a Chaos Orb, a little better. The plan is the old "essence spam" technique: what decides it is the price of the essence against a Chaos Orb's 1 Chaos.

These chances are now pinned against the emulator's own count of the target met, 24,000 uses per essence (`tests/fixtures/coe_recorded/b1_core.json`, cases `essence-r08-fear`, `essence-r09-spite`, `essence-r13-hatred`; `tests/test_coe_recorded_b1_core.py`).

### 3. The 74% rests on prices of lines that hardly trade (OR-8, #693)

| essence | price in the 2026-10-31 snapshot | price in the 2026-11-10 snapshot | the trade site's book that day |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shrieking Essence of Fear | 0.3425 c, previous league's median | 0.3333 c, poe.ninja, 0.5 units a day | 8.6 c, 11 units |
| Screaming Essence of Spite | 0.3399 c, previous league's median | 0.25 c, poe.ninja, 1.3 units a day | 2.8 c, 8 units |
| Deafening Essence of Hatred | 7.76 c, poe.ninja | 19.12 c, poe.ninja, 22.5 units a day | 13.2 c, 23 units |

At 0.33 c a use the essence costs a third of a Chaos Orb for the same chance, hence the 74%. Priced at what the trade book asks, the essence is dearer than a Chaos Orb and the plan moves away from it: with every thin essence at its trade median the plans cost 55.34 c (R08) and 49.90 c (R09) against 73.91 c and 70.06 c with fossils (measured in the comment of 2026-11-08 on #627, `actions_key.py` on this PC, 14 s each), a saving of 25% and 29%, not 74%.

Which price a plan buys at when poe.ninja's line trades under one unit a day is the open question #693 (OR-8: "a price is the mean of the cheapest few while the market has enough offers"); it is not a defect of the essence model, and this ticket does not decide it.

### 4. R13: real

Deafening Essence of Hatred forces the tier 1 Cold Resistance the target asks, on a line with 22.5 units a day, so a plan that uses it on a rare belt for one of the two resistances is the well-known essence step, priced from a deep line. The 6% saving is a real find of the planner, not an artefact.

## What this means for #405

As far as #627 goes, #405 may proceed: the plans the key hides are legal crafts the planner models correctly, so taking the key out shows true plans (OR-5). Once the key is out, R08's and R09's means are only as good as the price of the thin essence lines, which #693 decides; the re-measure of #405 should say which price rule stood.

How an answer tells the user "a kind the target left out is cheaper" is #405's own place (`cli/_offers.py` `kinds_named`, the "left out" line of #459, OR-13): with the key gone there is nothing left out to tell.
