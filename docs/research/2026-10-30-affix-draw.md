# Affix draw algorithm in 3.29

Ticket: [#6](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/6) (map [#1](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/1); evidence rule: [#2](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/2) Q2). Name `affix-draw`, claim prefix `AFX`. Date 2026-10-30. Patch 3.29.3.3 (repoe-fork export of 2026-10-19: `mods.json` sha256 `defbba31b60f9191080c78197278057f834f7f927c1a2a080a7ca6ed3ea686b5`, `base_items.json` sha256 `48afb07a5f40eb51552f90c17d53186469db282a2e759dab7b46e7f9d2a0f58b`). Every claim below was checked on 2026-10-30.

## Answer

One random explicit modifier is drawn like this. Candidates are the mods whose `domain` equals the base's `domain` and whose `generation_type` is `prefix` or `suffix` (AFX-01, AFX-02). A candidate is dropped when its `required_level` is above the item level (AFX-03, AFX-04), when it shares any family (`groups` entry) with a mod already on the item (AFX-07, AFX-08), or when its side is already full: magic 1 prefix + 1 suffix, rare 3 + 3, jewels of every kind 2 + 2 (AFX-09 to AFX-14). Its spawn weight is the weight of the first entry of the mod's own ordered `spawn_weights` list whose tag the item has (item tags = base tags plus the item's influence tag); weight 0 removes it (AFX-05, AFX-15, AFX-16). A candidate's chance is its weight over the sum of all remaining candidates' weights (AFX-06). Mods that carry `generation_weights` (365 attack mods) get a second, ordered first-match list that makes Sceptres, Staves, Rune Daggers and Wands roll them less often (AFX-17). The rolled values are uniform over the integers `min..max` of the chosen mod record, per stat (AFX-18, AFX-19). Ordinary currency on an item without Memory Strands is not tier-gated (AFX-22). **Not admitted, and blocking the primitive:** whether prefixes and suffixes are drawn from one pooled sum or by a side choice first (O-01); the numeric effect of `generation_weights` (O-02). Also open: `adds_tags` semantics (O-03), independence of hybrid stat rolls (O-04), a 0.8.3 note that rare rolls lean low (O-05).

Method: wiki pages read in the built-in browser pane (text and raw wikitext); official 3.26.0 notes via WebFetch; datamined records queried with Python on the pinned export (scripts in `.claude/scratch/research-affix-draw/`, main checkout, not committed). Datamined citations are `https://repoe-fork.github.io/<file>` plus record id.

## Admitted claims

Format: rule; source kind; URL; quote; patch the source speaks for. "Support" lines are extra sources for the same rule.

**AFX-01 Domain match.** A base draws random explicit mods only from mods whose `domain` equals the base's own `domain`. In the export both fields use one vocabulary: weapons, armour, rings, amulets, belts, quivers `item`; basic Jewels `misc`; Abyss Jewels `abyss_jewel`; Cluster Jewels `affliction_jewel`; flasks `flask`; tinctures `tincture`.
- Source kind: wiki. URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Modifier#Mechanics_overview. Quote: "Mod domain governs the type of entity the modifier is going to appear and may confer some specific rules (such as the number of affixes)". Patch: 3.29 (page edited 2026-10-10; newest Version-history row 3.28.0).
- Support, datamined (3.29.3.3): `base_items.json` `Metadata/Items/Armours/Helmets/HelmetStr1` (Iron Hat) `domain: "item"`; `Metadata/Items/Jewels/JewelStr` (Crimson Jewel) `domain: "misc"`; `mods.json` `IncreasedLife9` `domain: "item"`, `ManaCostEfficiencyJewel` `domain: "misc"`. Class counts: Helmet bases 96 `item`; Jewel bases 5 `misc` + 3 `affliction_jewel`; AbyssJewel 4 `abyss_jewel`. The wiki's numeric domain ids (Item 1, Jewel 10, Abyss Jewel 13, Cluster Jewel 21) are not in the export; the join is on the two `domain` strings, so no mapping is needed.

**AFX-02 Generation type.** The mods an ordinary random add can pick have `generation_type` `prefix` or `suffix`; the value is also the side (slot kind) the mod occupies. Other generation types (`unique`, `corrupted`, `essence`, `crucible_tree`, `searing_exarch_implicit`, ...) never enter this draw.
- Source kind: wiki. URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Modifier#Terminology. Quote: "Affixes are explicit modifiers that appear on magic and rare entities." Patch: 3.29 (as AFX-01).
- Support, wiki https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Modifier#Mechanics_overview: "Mod generation type determines how the modifier is generated, for example whether it's a prefix, suffix, corrupted or always applied (like on unique items)".
- Support, datamined `mods.json`: domain `item` has 2,246 `prefix` and 2,123 `suffix` records (4,369), 2,725 of them with a positive spawn weight on some tag. 458 records have `is_essence_only: true` (example `StrengthEssence7_`, `generation_type: "suffix"`, `spawn_weights: [{"tag":"default","weight":0}]`): weight 0 on every base, so ordinary currency never draws them.

**AFX-03 Level gate.** A candidate is eligible only if `required_level` <= item level (inclusive). Equal level is eligible.
- Source kind: wiki. URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Item_level#What_item_level_affects. Quote: "Magic and Rare items can only receive affixes which require a level less than or equal to the item's item level." Patch: 3.29 (page edited 2026-09-30; no version table).
- Support, wiki same page: "The highest item level required for any affix is 86." Datamined agrees: the highest `required_level` among domain-`item` prefix/suffix records with a positive weight is 86 (`WeaponElementalDamageOnWeapons6`, `MovementVelocity6`, `LocalIncreasedWardPercent8`).
- Support, datamined boundary: `ColdResist8` (`required_level: 84`, spawn `armour 1000, ring 1000, amulet 1000, belt 1000, quiver 1000, default 0`). Iron Hat pool (AFX-05 example below): item level 83 gives 40 prefixes and 78 suffixes; item level 84 gives 41 and 81.
- Support, wiki https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Modifier#Mod_Level: the level gate is not applied to mods "directly given to other entities, such as implicit mods"; this concerns granting, not the random draw.

**AFX-04 Jewel levels need no special case.** Basic Jewel (`misc`) mods have no item-level gate; this is carried by the data (every drawable `misc` prefix/suffix record has `required_level: 1`), so AFX-03 applies unchanged. Cluster Jewel (`affliction_jewel`) records have `required_level` 1, 50, 68, 73, 75, 78 or 84 and Abyss Jewel (`abyss_jewel`) records 1 to 86, so the gate applies to them.
- Source kind: wiki. URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Jewel#Basic_jewels. Quote: "No item level requirement". Patch: 3.29.0 (page edited 2026-08-07; newest row 3.29.0).
- Datamined (decides over wiki text, evidence rule): `mods.json`, 145 drawable `misc` prefix/suffix records, `required_level` set {1}; 504 drawable `affliction_jewel` records, levels {1, 50, 68, 73, 75, 78, 84}; 497 drawable `abyss_jewel` records, levels 1 to 86. The sentence on the Item level page "Non-Abyss Jewels are the one exception" is therefore imprecise for Cluster Jewels; the data rule is used.

**AFX-05 Spawn weight: first match in the mod's own ordered list.** The spawn weight of a mod on an item is the `weight` of the first entry of that mod's `spawn_weights` list (in the list's order) whose `tag` is in the item's tag set. Weights of other matching entries are ignored (never summed). Weight 0 removes the mod. The list order is the mod's, not the item's tag order.
- Source kind: wiki. URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Modifier#Spawn_weight_tag. Quote: "If multiple tags apply, the left-most tags are most important, where the right most tags are the least." Patch: 3.29 (as AFX-01).
- Support, wiki same section: "Match the Tags against the mod's list of tags." and "If the spawn weighting is zero, discard the mod, otherwise use it." The section's worked example (Glorious Plate against `LocalIncreasedPhysicalDamageReductionRatingPercent7`) is generated by a wiki query ordered by the mod's `spawn_weights.ordinal` (raw wikitext of `Modifier`).
- Support, datamined (reproduces the wiki example): `LocalIncreasedPhysicalDamageReductionRatingPercent7` (`required_level 84`, `groups ["DefencesPercent"]`) `spawn_weights [(str_armour,1000),(default,0)]`; Glorious Plate (`BodyStr17`, tags `str_armour, top_tier_base_item_type, body_armour, armour, default`) gets 1000, Assassin's Garb (`BodyDex17`, `dex_armour, ...`) matches only `default` and gets 0.
- Support, datamined, why the mod's order (not the item's tag order) is the rule: I evaluated every one of the 204 distinct base tag signatures of released `item`-domain bases against all 4,369 item prefix/suffix records (891,276 pairs). No pair lacks a matching entry (every record has a `default` entry and every base has the `default` tag), so a no-match rule is never exercised. The two readings differ on 2,954 pairs. Decisive case: `SpellDamageOnWeapon1` `spawn_weights [(attack_dagger,0),(focus,1500),(wand,1500),(sceptre,1500),(dagger,1500),(default,0)]`; Glass Shank (Dagger, tags `dagger, attack_dagger, one_hand_weapon, onehand, weapon, default`) must get 0, which only the mod-order reading gives (tag-order would hit `dagger` first and give 1500); Imperial Skean (Rune Dagger, no `attack_dagger`) gets 1500; Imbued Wand gets 1500 via `wand`. The wiki states the intended result at https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Dagger : "The first kind cannot roll caster-related affixes, and Rune Daggers can roll all affixes." (page edited 2026-07-20, before the 3.29.0 launch on 2026-07-24; no 3.29 note touches Daggers).
- Further datamined examples (reproduce the prototype's table): `Strength1` `spawn_weights [ring 1000, amulet 1000, belt 1000, str_armour 1000, str_dex_armour 500, str_int_armour 500, str_dex_int_armour 333, sword 500, mace 1000, sceptre 500, staff 500, axe 500, default 0]` (13 entries, sum 8333): Iron Hat gives 1000, Crude Bow gives 0, a str/dex base gives 500 (this is the 3.26.0 "lower chance to roll Attribute modifiers", see AFX-17). `IncreasedLife9` `[fishing_rod 0, boots 0, gloves 0, weapon 0, ring 0, amulet 0, default 1000]`: Iron Gauntlets (tags `str_armour, gloves, armour, default`) gets 0 even though `default` is 1000, Iron Hat gets 1000. `LocalIncreasedEnergyShield11` `[shield 0, helmet 0, gloves 0, boots 0, int_armour 1000, str_dex_int_armour 166, default 0]`: Simple Robe (`int_armour`) gets 1000.

**AFX-06 Draw probability.** Among the candidates that survive AFX-03, AFX-05, AFX-07, AFX-08 and AFX-14, the chance of one mod is its effective weight divided by the sum of the effective weights of all surviving candidates in the pool that is drawn from. Different tiers of one family are separate candidates (separate records, separate weights) until a tier is drawn; the pool scope (one pool across prefixes and suffixes, or not) is **not** admitted, see O-01.
- Source kind: wiki. URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Weight#Overview. Quote: "if a modifier with a weight of 1000, and a total weight of 40,000, that modifier has a 2.5% chance to roll (1000/40,000)". Patch: 3.29 (page edited 2026-03-19; no version table).
- Support, same page: the list of systems that use weights includes "All methods of non-deterministically rolling or crafting modifiers".
- Worked arithmetic, datamined weights, pooled reading (fixture only, depends on O-01): Iron Hat (`str_armour, helmet, armour, default`), item level 84, empty normal item: prefix pool 41 mods, total weight 39,275; suffix pool 81 mods, total 75,400. `IncreasedLife9` (weight 1000): 1000/114,675 = 0.872% pooled, 1000/39,275 = 2.546% if only the prefix pool is drawn from. The whole `IncreasedLife` family (10 tiers x 1000) is 8.72% pooled. With a mod of group `IncreasedLife` on the item: prefix pool 31 mods, 29,275.

**AFX-07 Family exclusion (restated from #2).** A candidate is removed if any of its `groups` equals any group of any mod on the item. A mod belongs to one or more families, and any shared family blocks. Exclusion is by `groups` (family), not by `type`.
- Source kind: wiki; also owner decision (#2, "Facts established during the round"). URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Modifier#Group/family. Quote: "Only one modifier from any given group may appear on an object; in other words, all modifiers belonging to a group are mutually exclusive with one another." Patch: 3.29 (as AFX-01); version history row 3.19.0: "Fixed a bug where Recombinators were ignoring modifier families in certain situations."
- Support, datamined: in domain `item`, 4,351 prefix/suffix records have 1 group and 18 have 2 (example `AddedFireDamageEnhancedMod`, `groups ["FireDamage","ConvertPhysicalToFire"]`); none of the 18 has a positive spawn weight, so every drawable ordinary mod has exactly one group. Present mods (crafted, fractured, veiled) can still have several. `type` is a different field: `LocalAddedFireDamageTwoHand1` has `type "LocalFireDamageTwoHand"`, `groups ["FireDamage"]`.

**AFX-08 Implicit mods do not block explicit mods.** An implicit modifier does not remove explicit candidates of its family from the draw.
- Source kind: wiki. URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Modifier#Version_history. Quote: "Implicit mods on items now do not stop explicit mods of the same type being spawned." Patch: 0.9.3 (no later row on the page reverses it; the page's newest row is 3.28.0).

**AFX-09 Magic capacity.** A magic item has at most 1 prefix and at most 1 suffix.
- Source kind: wiki. URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Rarity#Rarities. Quote: "Magic items have the same base statistics as a normal item of the same type, but have magical modifiers on them given by affixes, up to one prefix and one suffix." Patch: 3.29 (page edited 2026-05-01; no version row touches affix limits; none of the 3.29.0 to 3.29.3b wiki patch-note pages does).
- Support, wiki https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Modifier#Explicit_modifiers: "Magic - random modifiers based on conditions: (0 to 1) prefix and (0 to 1) suffix".

**AFX-10 Rare capacity (equipment).** A rare item has at most 3 prefixes and at most 3 suffixes (6 affixes).
- Source kind: wiki. URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Rarity#Rarities. Quote: "Rare items function the same as magic items, except they have at most six affixes – up to three prefixes and up to three suffixes." Patch: 3.29 (as AFX-09).

**AFX-11 Rare basic Jewel capacity.** A rare basic Jewel has at most 2 prefixes and 2 suffixes (4 affixes).
- Source kind: wiki. URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Jewel#Basic_jewels. Quote: "Rare items can only roll 4 affixes, 2 prefixes and 2 suffixes each". Patch: 3.29.0 (page edited 2026-08-07).
- Support, wiki https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Modifier#Explicit_modifiers: rare items 1 to 3 of each "with the exception of some items like jewels, which can only have (1 to 2) prefixes and (1 to 2) suffixes".

**AFX-12 Rare Abyss Jewel capacity.** A rare Abyss Jewel has at most 2 prefixes and 2 suffixes by default (corrupted or Allflame-crafted exceptions exist and are out of this draw).
- Source kind: wiki. URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Abyss_jewel#Mechanics. Quote: "Typically, like regular jewels, rare Abyss Jewels can hold a maximum of 2 prefixes and 2 suffixes, for a total of 4 affixes." Patch: 3.29 (page edited 2026-08-01, after the 3.29.0 Abyss revamp).

**AFX-13 Rare Cluster Jewel capacity.** A rare Cluster Jewel has at most 4 affixes in total. The split between prefixes and suffixes is not stated (O-09).
- Source kind: wiki. URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Cluster_Jewel (lead paragraph, no section anchor). Quote: "Like all jewels, rare cluster jewels can have a maximum of four affixes". Patch: 3.29 (page edited 2026-08-26).

**AFX-14 A full side closes.** When an item holds the maximum for a side, no candidate of that `generation_type` is eligible for the next add; the remaining side(s) form the whole pool.
- Source kind: wiki. URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Rarity#Rarities. Quote: "A magic item with only either a prefix or suffix property can have a second property of the missing type added to it by using an Orb of Augmentation". Patch: 3.29 (as AFX-09). This is the consequence of AFX-09/10/11/12 ("up to") that the wiki spells out for the magic case; the general form follows from the capacity numbers.

**AFX-15 Influence tags.** An influenced item has, in addition to its base tags, one influence tag per influence, named `<class key>_<influence key>`; a mod's `spawn_weights` may list such tags. Influence keys: Shaper `shaper`, Elder `elder`, Crusader `crusader`, Hunter `basilisk`, Redeemer `eyrie`, Warlord `adjudicator`. Class keys in the export (23): `2h_axe, 2h_mace, 2h_sword, amulet, axe, belt, body_armour, boots, bow, claw, dagger, gloves, helmet, mace, quiver, ring, rune_dagger, sceptre, shield, staff, sword, wand, warstaff`.
- Source kind: wiki. URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Modifier#Terminology. Quote: "Refers to Shaper influenced items, which have the "shaper" tag corresponding to the item class." Patch: 3.29 (as AFX-01). The same table gives the other five names (Hunter "basilisk", Redeemer "eyrie", Warlord "adjudicator", Crusader "crusader", Elder "elder").
- Support, datamined `mods.json`: 138 distinct influence-style tags occur in domain-`item` prefix/suffix `spawn_weights` (23 class keys x 6 influences, none missing); none of them is a tag of any base in `base_items.json`. Which class key belongs to which item class is not in a prose source (O-07).

**AFX-16 Influence mods are drawable only on items with their influence tag; ordinary mods are unaffected by influence.** 1,446 domain-`item` prefix/suffix records have positive weight only on influence tags (their last entry is `default 0`); none mixes influence and non-influence positive tags; none of the 1,279 ordinary records (some positive non-influence tag) names an influence tag, even with weight 0, so adding an influence adds candidates and leaves every ordinary weight unchanged. The 487 records that name influence tags with all weights 0 are disabled mods.
- Source kind: datamined. URL: https://repoe-fork.github.io/mods.json, e.g. record `AreaOfEffectSupportedUber1_` (`domain item`, `generation_type prefix`, `required_level 68`, `groups ["AreaOfEffect"]`, `spawn_weights [(helmet_shaper,800),(default,0)]`, text "(7-9)% increased Area of Effect / Socketed Gems are Supported by Level 16 Increased Area of Effect"). Count by first positive tag: elder 276, shaper 304, crusader 232, eyrie 198, adjudicator 233, basilisk 203. Patch: 3.29.3.3.
- Support, wiki https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Rarity#Rarities: "The types of modifiers that can be found or added to an item depend on its item level, the base type and any additional flags on the item (such as shaped or elder)." No tag for the Eldritch (Exarch/Eater), fractured, synthesised, corrupted or Memory-Strand state occurs in any domain-`item` prefix/suffix `spawn_weights` (`tags.json` has no `searingexarch`/`eaterofworlds` tag, and no tag containing `exarch` or `eater` is used by any mod), so those states do not change the pool.

**AFX-17 `generation_weights` (structure and affected classes; numeric effect open, O-02).** Attack mods (`adds_tags` contains `has_attack_mod`; 365 of the 553 domain-`item` prefix/suffix mods with that tag) carry a second ordered list `generation_weights`, identical on all 365: `[attack_dagger 100, dagger 50, warstaff 100, staff 50, sceptre 50, wand_can_roll_caster_modifiers 50, default 100]`. Tags are matched first-match in the mod's order (as AFX-05). Result: generic Daggers (`attack_dagger`) and Warstaves (`warstaff`, listed before `staff`) get 100; Rune Daggers (`dagger`), Staves (`staff`), Sceptres and Wands (`wand_can_roll_caster_modifiers`) get 50; every other base (Bows, Swords, armour, jewellery, ...) gets `default` 100. First match, not a product, is the only reading consistent with the official note below, because Warstaff bases carry both `warstaff` and `staff`. The test is the tag, not the item class: three released `experimental_base`, `not_for_sale` bases of class Warstaff (Capacity Rod, Potentiality Rod, Eventuality Rod) carry `staff` but not `warstaff` and so get 50.
- Source kind: official. URL: https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3787013 (Content Update 3.26.0, section "Item Changes"; also transcribed at https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Version_3.26.0). Quote: "Sceptres, Staves, Rune Daggers and Wands now roll Attack Modifiers less often (Warstaves and Attack Daggers are unaffected by this change)." Patch: 3.26.0 (2025-06-13); the 3.29.0 to 3.29.3b notes contain no change to it.
- Support, datamined: `mods.json` `LocalIncreasedPhysicalDamagePercentAndAccuracyRating1` (`spawn_weights [(weapon,1000),(default,0)]`, `adds_tags ["has_attack_mod"]`, the list above); base tags in `base_items.json`: Glass Shank `[dagger, attack_dagger, ...]`, Imperial Skean `[top_tier_base_item_type, dagger, ...]`, Imbued Wand `[wand_can_roll_caster_modifiers, wand, ...]`, Driftwood Sceptre `[sceptre, sceptre, ...]`, Gnarled Branch `[small_staff, staff, ...]`, Maelstrom Staff `[warstaff, top_tier_base_item_type, staff, ...]`. The same 3.26.0 note ("Weapons and Armour with multiple Attribute Requirements now have a lower chance to roll Attribute modifiers.") is realised inside `spawn_weights` (`Strength1`: `str_armour 1000`, `str_dex_armour 500`, `str_dex_int_armour 333`), so it needs no extra rule.

**AFX-18 Rolled values are uniform over the integers in the range.** For a stat whose minimum and maximum differ, the rolled value is one of the integers `min..max` (both ends included), each with equal chance. A stat with `min == max` is fixed. Stat values are integers in the export's raw units (display scaling such as permyriad to percent is cosmetic).
- Source kind: wiki. URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Modifier#Mechanics_overview. Quote: "the actual value of the stat in the cases where minimum and maximum are not the same will be randomized within with equal chances, so for example a Stat with a minimum value of 1 and a maximum value of 3, can either be 1, 2 or 3 at 1/3 chance for each value". Patch: 3.29 (as AFX-01). Caveat: see O-05 (0.8.3 line).
- Support, datamined: all 5,835 `stats` entries of domain-`item` prefix/suffix records have integer `min` and `max` and `min <= max`; 1,246 are fixed. Example `LifeLeechPermyriadSuffix1`: text "(0.2-0.4)% of Physical Attack Damage Leeched as Life", stat `life_leech_from_physical_attack_damage_permyriad` `min 20 max 40`.

**AFX-19 The range belongs to the mod record (tier); every stat has its own range.** A tier is one `mods.json` record; its stats list gives one `min`/`max` per stat id, and the draw picks the record first (AFX-05/06), then rolls each stat inside that record's ranges. Re-rolling values keeps the tier.
- Source kind: datamined. URL: https://repoe-fork.github.io/mods.json, e.g. `LocalIncreasedPhysicalDamagePercentAndAccuracyRating1` `stats [(local_physical_damage_+%,15,19),(local_accuracy_rating,16,20)]`; `LocalAddedFireDamage1` `stats [(local_minimum_added_fire_damage,1,2),(local_maximum_added_fire_damage,3,4)]` (text "Adds (1-2) to (3-4) Fire Damage"); domain `item` prefix/suffix stat counts per record: 1 stat 3,073 records, 2 stats 1,127, 3 stats 168, 4 stats 1. Patch: 3.29.3.3.
- Support, wiki https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Divine_Orb#Crafting. Quote: "A Divine Orb will not change the tier of the affix; it will only re-roll within its current tier." Patch: 3.29 (page edited 2026-07-26; newest row 3.28.0). Whether the stats of one record roll independently is O-04.

**AFX-20 Catalyst quality does not change spawn weights.** Item quality from catalysts is not an input of the pool.
- Source kind: wiki. URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Exalted_Orb#Version_history. Quote: "Quality from catalyst no longer affect the spawn weight thus the help text of this currency has also changed." Patch: 3.15.0 (page edited 2026-07-26; no later row reverses it).

**AFX-21 The 3.29 export contains the 3.29.0 mod changes (data currency check).** The Jewel mod added in 3.29.0 is present in the pinned export, so the datamined numbers used above are 3.29 numbers.
- Source kind: datamined, checked against wiki. URL: https://repoe-fork.github.io/mods.json record `ManaCostEfficiencyJewel` (`domain misc`, `generation_type suffix`, `required_level 1`, `spawn_weights [(not_int,300),(default,500)]`, stat `base_mana_cost_efficiency_+%` `min 10 max 15`). Wiki quote, https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Jewel#Version_history : "Added a new Modifier that can roll on Jewels, providing 10-15% increased Mana Cost Efficiency." Patch: 3.29.0.

**AFX-22 Items without Memory Strands are drawn by the ordinary rules.** In 3.29, Memory Strands (introduced 3.26.0) make ordinary currency favour higher tiers and consume strands; an item with no Memory Strands, and no Foulborn currency used on it, is not tier-gated. The planner start states in #2 Q6 (fresh normal base) carry no strands.
- Source kind: wiki. URL: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Memory_strand#Mechanics. Quote: "When crafting on an item with memory strands, it will consume a random amount of it to increase the tier of the modifier it adds to the item." Patch: 3.29 (page edited 2026-10-10; newest row 3.28.0). The formula the page gives is a community model, not admitted (O-06).

## Open (not admitted)

**O-01 Prefix and suffix: one pooled sum, or a side first? (BLOCKS the primitive.)** Is one add drawn from all surviving prefixes and suffixes together (chance = weight / sum over both sides) or is the side chosen first (for example 50/50) and then a mod within that side? Known: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Weight#Overview says the weight "is compared to the sum of all outcomes' weights" and that all methods of rolling modifiers use weights, which reads as one pool but does not mention sides; the Exalted Orb page says only "Exalted Orbs add a random explicit modifier to a rare item". The prototype sheet `affix_generation.md` says pooled, "does not first flip a fair prefix/suffix coin", sourced from Craft of Exile code: not admitted. No GGG statement found (WebSearch, several queries); no experiment found. Contrast on the wiki's Recombinator page ("Prefix and suffix pools do not mix and are independent") is recombinator-specific. What would settle it: a GGG staff statement, or an experiment: on one base with unequal side totals, record which side the single mod lands on. Iron Hat at item level 84: pooled predicts prefix 34.3% (39,275/114,675), a side coin predicts 50%; about 300 single-mod adds on a fresh normal base separate them by more than 5 sigma. Impact: every add on an item with both sides open; also all-slots Chaos/Alchemy fills. Recommended interim: an `owner` decision for the pooled reading, since it is the plain reading of the Weight page, until an experiment exists.

**O-02 Numeric effect of `generation_weights`. (Blocks attack mods on Wands, Sceptres, Staves, Rune Daggers only.)** Admitted: which bases are affected and that matching is first-match (AFX-17). Not admitted: that the value is a percentage that multiplies the spawn weight (effective weight = spawn weight x value / 100). Known: the official note says only "less often"; the data values are 100 and 50; the prototype's PoB/CoE reading is x(value/100) (not evidence). Illustration under that reading, item level 84, share of drawn mods that are attack mods: Imbued Wand 33.2% without, 20.1% with; Driftwood Sceptre 39.3% / 24.6%; Rune Dagger (Imperial Skean) 45.7% / 29.7%; Staff (Gnarled Branch) 32.9% / 19.8%; Dagger, Bow, Warstaff unchanged. What would settle it: a GGG statement of the factor, or an experiment (about 300 first-mod draws on an item-level-84 Wand separate 33.2% from 20.1% by about 5 sigma; do it after O-01 is settled because both change the same share). Alternative: owner decision to adopt x(value/100).

**O-03 `adds_tags` semantics.** Hypothesis: the `adds_tags` of mods present on the item join the item's tag set when later candidates are resolved (AFX-05). Datamined facts: 52 distinct adds-tags; `has_attack_mod` (2,299 records) and `has_caster_mod` (1,370) appear in no `spawn_weights` and no `generation_weights` entry at all, so for them the only consumers are the metamod stats `item_generation_cannot_roll_attack_affixes` / `item_generation_cannot_roll_caster_affixes` (crafted records `IntMasterItemGenerationCannotRollAttackAffixes`, `StrDexMasterItemGenerationCannotRollCasterAffixes`; belongs to the bench-metamods ticket). The tags consumed by item prefix/suffix `spawn_weights` are `grants_2h_support` (44 records, always at index 0 with weight 0, e.g. `SupportedByOnslaughtWeaponUber1` `[(grants_2h_support,0),(2h_sword_elder,400),...]`), `grants_crit_chance_support` (4 records, index 0, weight 0) and `deepwater_sword`; the only drawable adders are `ChanceToMaimUber1/2` (`grants_2h_support`, group `LocalChanceToMaim`, Shaper tags) and the four `CriticalStrikeChance...SupportedUber1/2` records (`grants_crit_chance_support`). The 4 `grants_crit_chance_support` consumers are those same four records (one family), so for them the tag adds nothing beyond AFX-07. The 44 `grants_2h_support` consumers have positive weight only on Shaper (12 tags) and Elder (6 tags) weapon tags and sit in groups `LocalPhysicalDamagePercent` (21), `WeaponSupportUber` (12), `WeaponCasterDamagePrefix` (9), `LocalChanceToMaim` (2): under the hypothesis, a Shaper weapon holding `ChanceToMaimUber1/2` cannot roll the other Shaper "Socketed Gems are Supported by" physical/spell-damage mods. So the semantics matter only for Shaper and Elder influenced weapons; for every item without influence it is moot. No prose source states the rule. Settle: a wiki or GGG statement; otherwise an `owner` decision to implement it as modelled, or to leave Shaper/Elder weapons out of the supported starts until then.

**O-04 Do the stats of one mod roll independently?** (hybrid mods, and the min/max pair of "Adds X to Y".) Known: each stat has its own range (AFX-19). The wiki sentence "Hybrid modifiers (modifiers with more than one line per stat) treat each line independently." (https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Modifier#Local) is in the local/global paragraph and does not speak about rolling, so it is not admitted for this. No GGG statement or sample found. Settle: GGG statement, or correlation over a few hundred rolls of one hybrid mod (for example `LocalIncreasedPhysicalDamagePercentAndAccuracyRating1`); perfect correlation versus independence is visible with about 100 samples. Impact: only targets that put a minimum roll on a stat of a multi-stat mod (#2 Q6); tier targets are unaffected.

**O-05 Does the 0.8.3 lean-low bias on rares still exist?** The same wiki page's version history says (0.8.3): "Mods on rares are now slightly biased towards the lower half of their ranges." The current prose (AFX-18) says equal chances; the wiki is internally inconsistent unless the bias was later removed, and no row records a removal. AFX-18 is admitted on the current prose; the owner may veto. Settle: GGG statement, or compare mean normalised roll of one wide-range mod on rare versus magic items (about 5,000 rolls each). Impact: roll-threshold targets only.

**O-06 Memory Strands and Modifier Tier Rating.** The page https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Modifier#Modifier_Tier_Rating says "The exact mechanics are not fully known, however there are three popular models"; the Memory strand formula `rollable_tiers = floor((100/(2.5 * strands + 147.5)) * (total_tiers - 1)) + 1` comes from a Reddit user and does not reproduce its own table at 0 strands (formula gives 5 for 8 tiers, table gives 8). Not admitted. Foulborn currency adds +75 MTR (wiki, same source). Scope consequence: any item with strands, and any use of Foulborn Exalted/Augmentation orbs or Orb of Remembrance, is outside the admitted draw; the start-state schema should hold strands = 0 (owner scope decision). Also unsourced: how often ordinary currency "consumes strands" matters only when strands > 0.

**O-07 Item class to influence class key.** Naming rule admitted (AFX-15), full table not: for example whether Thrusting One Hand Swords use `sword`, whether Fishing Rods have none. `mods_by_base.json` groups several classes per influence list and does not give a clean per-class tag. Settle: a wiki list or an export of the game's class-to-tag table; low impact for B1 (influence not in B1 benchmarks).

**O-08 Multi-mod fills and the count of mods.** Sequential draws without replacement with recomputed totals (Chaos/Alchemy/Regal/Essence fills), the 4/5/6 count rule (owner-accepted 8:3:1, #2) and the table rows for other cap sums (prototype: 2.5, 2, 1.5, 1) are not in this ticket; they belong to the basic-currency ticket. The single-add rule here is their building block.

**O-09 Capacity details outside this ticket.** Cluster Jewel per-side split (AFX-13); capacity of flasks, tinctures, maps, Talisman; how crafted, fractured, veiled and Eldritch-implicit slots count toward the 3 + 3 (bench-metamods, fracturing, veiled tickets). The metamod `PvPMasterLevel28Crafting` ("Cannot roll Modifiers with Required Level above 28", stat `item_generation_local_maximum_mod_required_level_override`) is a level override on AFX-03; bench ticket.

**O-10 League-state tags in 3.29 data.** `necropolis_body_armour`, `necropolis_boots`, `necropolis_helmet`, `necropolis_gloves` (51 records) and `deepwater_sword` (44 records) appear in item `spawn_weights` but come from league mechanics (Necropolis gravecrafting, Deepwater/Allflame) outside the Q7 action set. Treat as always absent.
