# Craft of Exile and wiki crafting pages: claim checklist

Ticket: [#27](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/27) (map #1). Date checked: 2026-10-30 for every source below. Domain: PoE 1, patch 3.29.3.3, ordinary mode.

## Answer

**248 claims** (116 general rules from the CoE explanation pages, CoE code and the wiki crafting pages, plus 132 metamod table cells). Backed by an admitted source: **177** (71 general, 106 cells). Contradicted: **29** (19 general, 10 cells). Only Craft of Exile says it: **40** (26 general, 14 cells). CoE gives no claim (2 blank cells): **2**. CoE is a cross-check only: its prose is current to patch 3.29.0.3 (its changelog, data update 2026-07-27, against the game at 3.29.3.3), and its metamod table, Basics and Advanced pages have the same text hash, and `poec_data.json`, `package.js` and `poec_common.json` the same SHA-256, as what the prototype read on 2026-09-20 (re-hashed in the browser on 2026-10-30). The datamine (repoe-fork 3.29.3.3) and the wiki show CoE wrong on: 17 stale essence entries (6 essence types), per-tag fossil stacking, the Awakener conflict rule, Orb of Conflict odds, the recombinator count table and item level, quality and catalyst rules, the multimod cost, the white-socket Vaal wording, and three removed Harvest crafts.

**Second pass (mapping to the B1 findings #6-#12).** 82 of the 89 rows that belong to a B1 ticket map to at least one B1 id (58 agree, 17 are open items in B1 that only CoE answers, 7 differed and were resolved, 7 have no B1 counterpart); of the 132 matrix cells 112 map to a B1 id (108 agree, 2 open, 2 differed). Nine conflicts with the B1 findings are listed in section 4 (rows 1 to 6 changed a CHK row: the scope of CHK-007, the corrections of CHK-026, CHK-030 and CHK-067, the formula split CHK-242, and the sources of the fossil-exclusive-mods row); section 5 lists what the checklist adds; section 6 is the merged list of CoE-only rules the owner has to admit or reject.

Verdict key: `backed` = an admitted source states the rule (source kind, URL, short verbatim quote, patch given in the row); `contradicted` = an admitted source states otherwise or says the thing no longer exists; `CoE only` = no admitted source states it. Admitted kinds: `official`, `wiki`, `datamined` (repoe-fork 3.29.3.3 export), `owner` (grill #2), `experiment`. CoE, PoB, poedb, guides and memory are never evidence here.

How the sources were read. CoE pages: built-in browser, `#content.innerText`, 2026-10-30; the live Craft of Exile pages were compared by hash with the cached copies (`.claude/scratch/research-coe-wiki-checklist/coe_pages_live_2026-10-30.md`). Wiki: built-in browser, MediaWiki `api.php?action=parse` of each current revision (revision numbers in the patch column). Version histories were read for Metamod, Modifier, the basic orbs, Essence, Fossil, Resonator, Harvest crafting, Crafting Bench and Fracturing Orb; for the B2-B4 pages (Awakener, Recombinator, Vaal, Quality, Catalyst, Beastcrafting, Eldritch, Veiled, Imprint, Synthesis) the mechanics text at the current revision was read, not the whole history. Official forum posts: pathofexile.com is behind a Cloudflare challenge in the browser pane (not bypassed), so the posts were read through WebFetch and the quoted sentences are the ones WebFetch returned. Datamine: Python over `repoe-3.29.3.3/*.json`; scripts and outputs are in the scratch folder. CoE code lines come from the cached `package.js` (hash-identical to the live file).

## 3. Mod groups: CoE against repoe-fork `groups`

What CoE claims: its Modgroups page (<https://www.craftofexile.com/modgroups>, read live 2026-10-30) lists the 38 modifiers that have more than one modgroup and says exclusion is by modgroup (Advanced page); each CoE modifier in `poec_data.json` carries `modgroups` (a list), which is what CoE blocks on. CoE data file: hash-identical to 2026-09-20 (patch 3.29.0.3 data); repoe-fork is 3.29.3.3, so differences can come from patches after 3.29.0.3.

Method. (a) Page-level: the 38 listed modifiers reduce to 24 distinct group-sets; repoe-fork `groups` of every prefix/suffix mod in domains item, crafted, unveiled, area give 31 distinct multi-group sets (scripts `grp_compare*.py` in the scratch folder). (b) Mod-level, forward: each CoE prefix/suffix modifier tier (`tiers[mod][base][].ilvl`) is matched to repoe mods with the same side, the same stat text with numbers replaced by `#`, and `required_level` = tier ilvl, restricted to equipment-type domains; the CoE `modgroups` set is compared with the set of repoe `groups`. (c) Mod-level, reverse: every repoe prefix/suffix mod of domains item, crafted, unveiled, veiled, delve is matched to CoE the same way. Matches are on text + level, not on a shared id (CoE has no repoe id), so a collision with a similar mod of another pool can show as a false difference; such cases are named below.

| Comparison | Agree | Differ | No counterpart |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| (a) multi-group sets on the CoE Modgroups page vs repoe multi-group sets | 24 of 24 CoE sets found in repoe | 0 CoE-only | 7 repoe sets not on the CoE page |
| (b) CoE modifiers -> repoe, group set equal | 2,331 | 7 (all collisions, see below) | 1,296 CoE modifiers (+330 without tiers): maps, watchstones, grafts, heist, cluster jewel, Incursion/Delve/Sentinel entries and removed mods |
| (c) repoe mods -> CoE, group set equal | 4,219 (4,160 single-group, 59 multi-group) | 38 | 2,170 repoe mods (item 1,201; crafted 722; delve 147; unveiled 78; veiled 22): mods newer than CoE data 3.29.0.3 or text/level mismatch |
