# The planner chooses its start base, and a fractured start, from a priced list kept fresh by the system

Ticket [#337](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/337), note [`docs/research/2026-11-01-buying-any-state.md`](../research/2026-11-01-buying-any-state.md), owner answers of 2026-11-01. This is the v1 slice. The general "buy any compressed state" mechanism stays a v2 hypothesis (map [#325](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/325)); a bought item replaces the item in hand, so every buy offer is a reset to a priced state, and `restart` (ADR-0011) is the one-candidate case.

## Decisions

1. **The target's item level is a floor.** The planner may plan at a lower item level than the file's number when every leaf of the target stays reachable. The floor is derived from the tiers the leaves ask for (tiers are absolute per base), or stated by the target. A level is not lowered where the author wrote it to mean "the top tier at that level" (R05's resistances). One plan per equal-pool group of levels; the answer is the cheapest total of start price plus plan, the other options listed with their totals. Measured: R13 5,492 / 5,638 / 6,311 Chaos at levels 84 / 85 / 86. Built in `targets/floor.py` (ticket #352): the floor is the lowest `required_level` among the mods that pass each requirement's tier, roll and `not_crafted`, from the pool of a draw, the bench and the essences the target's `actions:` leaves in; the author's keys are `item_level_floor` (raises the floor) and `tiers_top_at_item_level` (no lowering, R05); R13 and R08 floor at 84, R05 at 75.
2. **The price source is a profile setting** (with the #323 inputs): `ninja`, `trade` or `both`, default `both`. The refresher fetches both. The planner prices with the trade data (instant buyout); when it is missing or stale it falls back to the poe.ninja base row (item levels 82 to 86, at least 5 observations) and marks the price so. The answer shows both prices side by side and flags a gap above 20% (configurable). This holds for bases and influence, not only fractured starts. A poe.ninja row does not prove an instant-buyout listing and the report says so. No price from the chosen source, no option.
3. **A fractured start (one wanted fractured mod) is priced from the trade data** the system keeps fresh (ADR-0007, amended). A candidate is a stat text of a target leaf that the base can roll, for one pool group of item levels; it is asked for only if an offered action can rework a rare item (R13 has none, so none is asked there). A market exists at **10 or more listings**; the price is the **mean of the 5 cheapest** usable instant-buyout listings.
4. **A bought rare carries at most 1 wanted mod** in v1.
5. **Both are configurable realism rails**: the listing-count threshold (default 10) and the maximum wanted mods (default 1). The planner reports, for every offer left out, which rail cut it and the value that cut it, so the two rails can be compared later.
6. **The recombinator trick for item level is not offered.** The output level cannot exceed the higher input, so the pair is dominated by buying the higher base.
7. Each start option is its own plan with its own restart (an upper bound on the joint optimum; the item level is fixed per abstraction). A joint restart is v2.

## Consequences

- No query is sent while a plan is made; the planner reads the dated price file (ADR-0007). A trade-priced option older than the file's 7 days is a warning, and a stale or missing one is not offered.
- Strands, quality and the extra crafted slot stay as ADR-0043, ADR-0049 and ADR-0053 decided; the fractured list is the place a priced start with one of them would join.
- Vendor-recipe starts (ADR-0059) join the same start-option list with a constructed price.

## Not decided here (v2, #325)

The general mechanism, a finishing-cost bound for routes that are not erasing rerolls, donors as buy offers (`Need`, ADR-0022), mods carried by a bought rare above the cap, SSF owned items, and the questions 6 to 9 of the note.

## Amendment: how the price source is read ([#357](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/357))

The setting is `price_source` (`ninja`, `trade`, `both`, default `both`) and the gap threshold is `price_gap_percent` (default 20), both in the `[planner]` table of `crafting.toml` (the profile, apart from the target), or `--price-source` for one run. `prices/price_source.py` `source_price` answers, per name: `ninja` uses the poe.ninja row only; `trade` uses `trade_market.offer` only (instant buyout, fresh, the listing-count rail met); `both` uses the trade price and, when `offer` says it is missing, stale, thin or unknown, the poe.ninja row, marked as a fallback with the reason. A hand override of the name wins under every setting. A fractured base has no poe.ninja row: under `ninja` it is not offered, under `both` it is priced by trade alone. The poe.ninja row of a name whose trade price covers it is kept in the price file's `ninja` table (a name that only has a poe.ninja row keeps it in `prices`), so the answer can show both prices side by side; the refresher (#356) writes that table. The gap is the dearer price over the cheaper, in percent, flagged when strictly above the threshold, and only under `both`. The start sources receive the setting in their `StartContext`; the plain start item and the recipe starts are priced from the price file as before.

## Amendment: the key `tiers_top_at_item_level` is gone ([#457](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/457))

Decision 1 above says a level is not lowered where the author wrote the tiers to mean "the top tier at that level", and names the key `tiers_top_at_item_level` for it. OR-1 makes that reading the default for every leaf with no `min_tier` (a leaf with a `mod:`, a roll or a stated value asks no tier), so the key and its floor kind `pinned` are removed from the parser, the model and the 11 target files that set it. The floor now asks the one judgement (`status_of`) of each source (the pool, the bench, a forced mod), at the mod's lowest or highest roll, so a leaf's top tier keeps the level at which that tier exists, and a bench craft or an essence-only mod, which has no natural tier, gives a tier leaf nothing. Whether a target whose guide states its item level should still not be planned lower is not decided here; the planner may now plan R05 and the other ten at the floor their tiers allow.

## Amendment: a thin market is priced, marked, and no longer "never offered" ([#718](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/718), OR-8)

At the end of the Allflame league (2026-11-08) a Muttering Essence of Hatred read 134,936 c from a book of 5 offers, every one a troll price. The rule that took the mean of the cheapest five took them all, and the #355 rule that cuts such a book (fewer than 10 listings, or fewer than 3 usable) made it "never offered", so the route page fell to the poe.ninja row with only the word "thin". The owner decided on 2026-11-08 (answer «в») that a thin market is priced, and always with a mark:

1. A market with enough offers keeps its price: the mean of the cheapest five (`DEFAULT_K`), unmarked. The numbers of #355 (`DEFAULT_MIN_LISTINGS` = 10, `MIN_USABLE` = 3) do not change.
2. A thin market is priced with the league's last deep price, marked **old** with its date (`old price, read on <date>, the market has few offers now`). It is the last search of this league that met the #355 rule, whatever its age; a reading of another league is never this league's.
3. When this league never had a deep reading, a thin market is priced with the mean of the cheapest listings there are (at most `k`), marked **thin** (`few offers: the mean of the N listings there are`).
4. A search that found no listing at all (total 0) is unchanged: OR-7's marked poe.ninja stand-in. So is a thin market with no listing that can be priced and no deep reading of this league: there is nothing to show, and the stand-in comes with its reason.

How it is kept. The price file gains a top-level `deep` table: per name, the last deep reading of its league (`chaos`, `league`, `date`, `listings`, `usable`). A deep reading (`store_trade_price`) writes it and replaces the older one; a thin or empty reading (`store_market`) leaves it as it is, and a file written before the table existed moves its trade price into the table when the first thin reading takes that price out of `prices`. A `thin` record in `markets` gains `chaos` and `used`, the mean it was read with. `merge_trade_prices` carries the table across a poe.ninja re-fetch of the same league and drops it, with a warning, for another league. `trade_market.offer` returns the price with `basis` (`old` or `thin`), the date and the mark; `price_source.source_price` puts the mark in `SourcedPrice.marks`, so the start text and the JSON print it where they print the fallback mark. A quoted price whose stored counts the listing-count rail given now calls thin (the rail was raised) is a thin price too: the mean of what there was, marked thin.

What the planner reads. Every price a plan is made from goes through one function, `trade_market.planned_entry(price_file, name)`: the start item (`cli/_plan.py` `resolve_start_price`), the start options' goods (`cli/_start_options.py`), the offers' inputs (`cli/_offers.py` `price_offers`) and the benchmark's missing-start-price check. A name with a price in `prices` is read as always (quoted trade price, hand override, poe.ninja, previous league, poe.watch). A name with only a `thin` record of the league reads as the rule says, as a `trade-api` price whose `source` carries the mark (`trade-api, old price, read on <date>, the market has few offers now`, or `trade-api, few offers: the mean of the N listings there are`). Every line that prints a source prints the mark with it: the start line, the cost breakdown, the JSON `price_source`. With nothing to show the answer is None, as before (the plan says "no price for the start item"; the start options use `source_price`, OR-7's marked stand-in). The planner does not judge age or rail, as it never did.

Only a reading that meets the default rail (`DEFAULT_MIN_LISTINGS`, `MIN_USABLE`) is stored as the league's deep reading, whatever `--min-listings` the run used; a legacy trade price is moved into the table only if its counts meet it. The table survives a re-fetch of the same league even where the new file prices the name; another league drops it with a warning.

The freshness window is unchanged: a thin record is fresh for 7 days, then `stale`, and the refresher asks again. The old price is shown however old its date, because its date is printed with it.

Not in this amendment: the bulk-exchange reader of the parked #662 (its reader goes through this rule, with units as the depth of a bulk book) and the numbers of #710.
