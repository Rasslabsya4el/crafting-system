# Big graphs: packed arrays first, then bounds and an envelope search; cuts are classified by what they guarantee, and fixed rails are never defaults

**Draft** for [ticket #312](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/312) (research note `docs/research/2026-11-01-exact-merging-and-cuts.md` holds the numbers; the three outside reports are `2026-11-01-dr-1-…`, `-dr-2-…`, `-dr-3-…`). Not accepted: the owner decides which of the tickets below are cut. The number 0063 may need to change if another draft takes it first.

R04 (177,242 states, 535.9 M transitions), R14 (816,153 states), the strand target S02 (106,002 states, 76.6 billion transitions) and R08 with the Alchemy did not solve exactly. The question was whether their states differ in detail no decision sees, so that an exact merge would shrink them. Measured, it depends on the route: S02 shrinks 5,300x (20 classes), R13 and S13 51x and 313x, R05 and R10 100x and more, R08 with the Alchemy 17x, but R04 only 2.0x and R14 1.4x. The measurement shows how much merges, not why: routes that type their junk as numbered family types merge 5,300x (S02) and 1.4x (R14) alike. What stopped R04 and R14 is the memory of the solver for the transitions (about 32 B each, 17 GB for R04), a serial Python walk, and the absence of any cut by cost before or during the walk. Solved on packed arrays after the exact merge, R04 = 69,716.06 chaos, R14 = 5,786.62 and S02 = 33,879 (1-chaos prices) in minutes. The values move with main (a second snapshot gives R04 = 69,257.54 and R14 = 5,784.49: the chances of the Exalted Orb and the essences changed in between), so the goldens are taken when the packed solver lands. The outside reports' verdict (the SSP model is right, the unit of computation is too concrete, optimise while generating) is borne out in one respect: the optimal policy of R04 and R14 reaches about a tenth of the reachable states (note, "The envelope"). It is not borne out in another: exact lumping alone does not shrink R04 or R14 much.

## Decision

1. **The first lever is the representation: packed arrays and a packed policy iteration are the production path for large graphs.** Transitions as numpy arrays (destination, chance, cost per action, kernels shared), self-loops eliminated into exit costs, policy iteration from a proper start policy (the shortest-path tree of the luckiest path lowers the distance to the goal with positive chance, so it is proper), each evaluation an iterative linear solve, the Bellman residual reported and certified the way `solver/_certify.py` certifies a component. The exact merge (the coarsest partition with the same goal status, action costs and outcome chances over the classes) is folded in as a pre-step because it costs little: 25 s on R04, and it cuts R04's edges 3.1x, which is what lets the arrays fit; on S02 it is the only way. The object solver stays for small routes and for everything with inputs, variants or Locks. **Acceptance:** the packed solve is cross-checked against the object solver on R05, R08, R10, R12 and R13 to 1e-9 relative (the research script is already within 3e-12 on them) before R04's and R14's values become goldens (taken on the main of that day).
2. **The walk is packed and parallel.** States and kernels as arrays instead of objects, and the walk split over processes. Coordinate with #250 (PR #326): its pool and `Abstraction.prime` survive; its BFS prefetch hook does not. Measured now: R04 245–457 s and 1.3 GB, R14 1,721–1,962 s and 8 GB, S02 828–1,156 s and 2.7 GB (all in the research walker, which keeps one record per kernel key; the production walk of R04 took 3,526 s and 6.3 GB in PR #270). **Targets by machine class:** on a 4-vCPU runner (16 GB) R14 under 10 minutes and 4 GB; on a weak PC with 8 GB, R04 is estimated to fit (walk 1.3 GB plus about 3 GB of packed arrays per kernel, not measured) and R14 is not possible today (8 GB for the walk alone), with a packed walk estimated at 1 to 2 GB (not measured).
3. **Bounds, then an envelope search.** A research ticket first: a lower bound that sees the junk mod types (pattern databases and cost partitioning from report 1 are the starting point). The prize is measured: a perfect bound would need 13% of R04's classes and 11% of R14's, and the cheap bounds tried (luckiest path, optimistic coarse models over status, rarity, mods per side, fractured ids) give 0.5–4.1% of the cost on them and cut nothing. Only when a bound reaches half of the exact cost on R04 and R14 is an iLAO*-style envelope search built, with executable upper-bound policies (a given route or a cheap first solve), per-state action elimination, and a stop when the root decision is proved.
4. **Cuts are classified by what they guarantee, and the report says which ones ran.** *Exact*: an action is not expanded when its cost with lower bounds exceeds the best action's cost with upper bounds (ADR-0031 raised from offers to states; nothing is lost). *Tolerance, only with a stated guarantee*: an outcome is lumped only when `p * (UB - LB)` is below the margin the bounds leave the choice; the report states `V_opt <= V* <= V_pess`, the relative gap and that every settled choice is unchanged (measured: every state settled on R05 and R10, none on R04). *Safety net*: a time or memory limit stops the run and says so, and the report lists the limits that were binding.
5. **Fixed rails and thresholds are not defaults.** A ceiling of 1,000 uses makes R11 (1,086 chaos) infinite; lumping outcomes below 1e-3 moves R13 from 6,300 to between 24 and 16,800 chaos and R08 with the Alchemy by +4.3%; 1e-4 and 1e-5 changed nothing on any route measured, but nothing says why. A user's own rails and inventories stay a user input (#323), and report 2's warning applies to them: with a hard budget in the state the stationary self-loop macro is no longer exact.
6. **An exact merge inside the walk is not built.** On R04 and R14 a canonical key would gain 2.0x and 1.4x at most. For S02, where it would save the 19-minute walk, none of the nine simple projections tried (on R08 and S02) is both exact and merging: dropping or coarsening the slots is not exact, keeping them merges nothing. A key would have to come from the partition itself (for instance from what `Reads` says the actions can read); that is a research ticket, not a build.
7. **The strand targets S02, S11 and S13 are in the tractability set** next to R04, R14 and R08 with the Alchemy (as today's loaders read them: strand words removed). S11 and S13 are 4,066 states and 13 classes; S02 is solved after the merge.

## What the measurements say about each part

- **Packed solve:** R04's merged graph (89,778 classes, 414,544 actions, 171 M edges) in 72 to 155 s; the research script's memory is 6.0 GB after loading and flattening and 14.4 GB at the end of the full cut analysis, so the production version must be leaner (per kernel, not per action). R14's (589,930 classes, 6.9 M edges) in 19 s; S02's (20 classes) under a second. The object solver died at 15.7 GB on R04.
- **Envelope:** the optimal policy reaches 11,767 of R04's 89,778 classes (at most 18,911 of 177,242 states, 10.7%) and 65,149 of R14's 589,930 (at most 87,271 of 816,153, 10.7%); with a 0.1% tolerance the envelope is already 78% of R04's states and 74% of R14's, with 1% all of R04's. Pairs: 11,766 of 414,544 on R04 and 65,149 of 1.92 M on R14; all offers of R04 and R14 appear in the envelope, so root pruning of offers removes none there.
- **Cuts:** the luckiest-path bound with 32 sweeps is 0.6% of R04's cost and cuts nothing; the coarse optimistic bounds give 97% on R13 but 1.4–3.5% on R04 and 0.5–4.1% on R14. Most actions not chosen are within 1% of the best (R04: 95%), so an exact cut cannot remove them, and a tolerance cut only with a settled margin.

## Consequences

- The proof sketch of the exact cut: `LB(s') <= V*(s') <= UB(s')` for every state; an optimistic model has an optimal value at most the exact one because a concrete policy is a policy of the model that picks the action of the member it is in; an action whose lower Q exceeds the best upper Q is in no optimal policy, so the optimal value of the cut graph is that of the whole.
- The packed solve is a second solver. Both must agree on the routes both can hold; a test keeps it so.
- A walk guided by bounds needs the bounds before the walk, so the bound must come from an abstraction that does not need the full graph (the variants level of ADR-0023 is the nearest existing piece).

## Proposed tickets (in this order)

1. **Packed solve with the exact merge** (`solver/`): the policy-iteration solve of `research/312/prune_analysis.py` and the partition refinement of `research/312/exact_classes.py`, per kernel, with certified residual; cross-checked to 1e-9 on R05, R08, R10, R12, R13; R04, R14 and S02 end to end through `make_plan`; goldens for R04 and R14. Done when R04, R14 and S02 give their exact value through the planner.
2. **Packed, parallel walk** (`abstraction/`, `cli/`): states and kernels as arrays, split over processes; coordinated with #250; targets per machine class above. Done when R14 is under 10 minutes and 4 GB on a 4-vCPU runner and the note says what an 8 GB PC does.
3. **A lower bound that sees the junk mod types** (research): pattern databases and cost partitioning, buildable without the full graph; measured on R04, R14 and S02 against the 13% / 11% perfect-bound prize. Done when a bound is at least half of the exact cost on R04 and R14, or a note says it cannot be.
4. **Envelope search** (build, after 3): iLAO*-style expansion from the start with the bound, an executable upper bound, per-state action elimination and a stop when the root decision is proved; measured against the full walk on R05, R13, R04, R14.
5. **Report the cuts and the limits** (`cli/`): the report lists which cut kinds ran, the tolerance and what it guarantees, and the limits that were binding; no cut by a fixed count or chance. Done when a binding time or memory limit makes the plan say "not exact, stopped at …" and a test covers it. Fits #323's user rails.
6. **An exact key in the walk** (research, optional): from `Reads`/`Adds`, checked against the partition on S02, R13 and R05. Not to be built before a key equals the partition there.

## Not proposed

A rail or a chance threshold with a default; lumping by a fixed chance; a canonical key for R04 and R14.
