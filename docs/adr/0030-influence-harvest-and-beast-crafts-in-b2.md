# Influence, Harvest and beast crafts in B2 follow the data, then Craft of Exile

B2's single-item additions that are not Eldritch, veiled or catalysts: the influenced Exalted Orbs, the Orb of Dominance, the Harvest add/remove and swap crafts, and the beasts. For each the research ([#86](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/86), [#88](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/88)) left points no source settles. [Grill B2](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/93) settled them by the evidence rule (ADR-0005, [#28](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/28)): where the owner knows, the owner's answer; where nothing speaks, Craft of Exile, and the claim carries `approximate` (ADR-0028).

## Influenced Exalted Orbs and the Orb of Dominance

- Six orbs (Shaper's, Elder's, Crusader's, Hunter's, Redeemer's, Warlord's) add their influence and one mod of it to a rare item with an open slot and item level 68 or more. The mod is drawn only from that influence's mods, through the shared draw (item level, family exclusion, weights, side locks, Attack/Caster blocks). They are refused on **every item that already has an influence, the orb's own included, and on an item with an Eldritch implicit** (the owner has not checked; an optional in-game check may change it, #86 O-01 and O-02), and on fractured and synthesised items. An empty pool refuses the orb.
- The **Orb of Dominance** (body armour, boots, gloves, helmet, with two influenced mods) removes one influenced mod and upgrades another by one tier; a top-tier mod becomes its Elevated version. Both picks are uniform (Craft of Exile, marked approximate). It respects the side locks and ignores the Attack/Caster blocks.
- **Beast influence recipes** add a mod drawn from that influence's pool, as its Exalted Orb would draw it (only the pool is borrowed: the item already has the influence); no source says which mod, so the claim is marked unverified and approximate.
- Randomise Influence and the Awakener's Orb are not here (the first is out of v1; the second is B3).

## Harvest add/remove

Eleven crafts (Fire, Cold, Lightning, Physical, Life, Defence, Chaos, Attack, Caster, Speed, Critical). The craft adds a mod of the tag, then removes **another** one. The addition is drawn **in proportion to the weight** among the tag's mods the item can roll at its item level; the removal is **uniform** among every other non-fractured mod on an unlocked side, crafted mods and metamods included (Craft of Exile, marked approximate). The addition obeys the side locks (open slots on a locked side can be filled) and the Attack/Caster blocks; the removal obeys the locks and ignores the blocks. It needs a non-influenced item and an open slot for the addition. It **works on items with an Eldritch implicit or a fractured mod** (the owner).

## Swaps

- **Resistance swaps** (six, 500 lifeforce of the target colour) change a resistance mod to another element. **The tier is kept and the value is rerolled inside the tier** (the owner); this holds for maximum-resistance swaps too. The required levels of the tiers differ by element, so **a swap into a tier above the item level is refused** (main chat). It respects the side locks.
- **Damage swaps** (six, 500 lifeforce and one Crystallised Rancour) follow Craft of Exile for **which mods qualify**, marked approximate; critical strike multiplier mods are excluded (wiki, since 3.28.0).

## Beasts

- **Wild Bristle Matron** adds one of the five metamods "randomly, only if allowed". Odds: **uniform among the allowed metamods** (main chat, marked approximate). It needs an open slot on the metamod's side and no crafted mod (except `Can have up to 3 Crafted Modifiers`); with the suffixes full and a prefix open the only allowed one is `Suffixes Cannot Be Changed`.
- **Farric Wolf Alpha and Lynx Alpha** add a prefix and remove a random suffix, or the reverse, on rare items. The added mod is gated by the **beast level**, not the item level: a setting, **default 83** when a target names none. "Any rare creature" slots cost 1 chaos each, also a setting.
- **Aspects** (Avian, Cat, Crab, Spider; level 20, or 30 with The Black Mórrígan) are offered only when a target names one. They add a suffix, need an open suffix, and an item holds one aspect; they are **not bench crafts, so they do not use the crafted-mod limit** (Craft of Exile, marked approximate).
- **Talisman fractures**: one fracture on a rare Talisman with at least 4 mods, two with at least 6, not on influenced or fractured items. **Which mods are fractured is uniform** (main chat, marked approximate). The Talisman base reroll is out of v1.

## Amendment: how the influence crafts are built ([#154](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/154))

`actions/influence.py` holds `InfluencedExaltedOrb(influence)`, `OrbOfDominance` and `BeastInfluenceRecipe(influence)`; their claims are `influence.*`, `dominance.*` and `influence_beast.*`. All three are `planner=False` until the compressed state can tell the influenced mods apart and tier an Elevated mod ([#155](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/155)); the contract suite and its kernel check already pass for them.

- **The pool.** An influenced Exalted Orb and a beast recipe draw through the shared pool, restricted to the mods whose deciding spawn tag is the influence's own tag (`harvest_reforge.influence_mod_is_influence_spawn`); the Shaper helmet pool at item level 100 is 11 prefix and 20 suffix records, weights 5,600 and 11,100. The orb is also refused below item level 68 and when no mod is legal (a refusal, nothing consumed).
- **Dominance** lists one result per ordered pair of influenced mods on unlocked sides, each 1/(n(n-1)), as `Finite`: a new mod is at its lowest values, so the value reroll of an Elevated mod is not shown. The next tier of a mod is taken from the mods of its own influence (the first spawn tag the item has decides), so on an Awakener item the Crusader maximum Mana is never the "next tier" of the Elder one. A mod it cannot name the upgrade of is `OutOfScopeError`: an influenced mod with no tier on the item, an ambiguous next tier, a top-tier mod with no Elevated version in the mapping, an influenced item with a fractured mod (no such item exists in the game).
- **The Elevated mapping** is data: `src/crafting_system/data/elevated.json`, made by `python -m crafting_system.data elevated` from Craft of Exile's table `maeven.bmods` read against the pinned export (`data/elevated.py`: by the text of the mods, else by their level and value ranges, else by the one Elevated mod of the family). It holds 206 top-tier mods of the 3.29.3.3 export; the 35 kinds of table entry (207 keys) it cannot link are listed in the file with the reason, because Craft of Exile's table is older than the export. It is for one export; the patch-update skill rebuilds it, and a test fails until it is.
- **Beast recipes** price the named red beast, a Craicic Maw and two rare beasts of any kind (the setting of `beastcraft.any_rare_creature_price`).

Origin: [Grill B2](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/93) (decisions list; owner round 1, point 4, items 3 to 5); [Influence crafts](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/86); [Harvest add/remove/swap and beasts](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/88); ADR-0013, ADR-0028.

## Amendment: the compressed state of the Harvest add, remove and swap crafts and of the beast crafts ([#149](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/149))

The seven kinds (`harvest-add-remove`, `harvest-resistance-swap`, `harvest-damage-swap`, `talisman-fracture`, `aspect-beast`, `wild-bristle-matron`, `beast-side-swap`) are planner kinds now, all `opt_in`: a target names them in `actions:`, because they make a state keep the junk (and the Matron makes it model the metamods), so no other target pays for them. An aspect is offered, on top of that, only for a target that wants it (`Adds.wanted_only`). Every action here answers with a `Finite` (a swap with its `RolledFinite`), so the kernel is the generic one of `Fixed` parts; what #149 added is what the state must keep for each action to be exact (layer 1 against the finished items of the action, `tests/test_abstraction_harvest_b2.py`, `tests/test_abstraction_beasts.py`, `tests/test_abstraction_b2_real.py`).

- **A swap keeps the tier, so its source families are told apart by mod.** The state forgot which tier of a family a junk mod was (a slot is a family or a family type), so a Lightning Resistance tier 1 and a tier 3 were one slot and the swap refused or answered for the wrong one. `Reads.by_mod` names the families whose slots are the kind `$<mod id>` (the mechanism of a Divine Orb's rolls); a swap names the families of the mods of the item domain that spawn and carry a stat of its claim for either element, 6 families for Fire to Cold resistance and 14 for Fire to Cold damage in 3.29.3.3, and also `families` the same set, so the families a swapped mod would clash with are in it.
- **A Harvest add draws by a tag; the family types carry it.** The new mod is drawn by weight among the mods of the pool with the craft's tag, so a held family matters through the weight and the tag of its mods. `Reads.draw_tags` makes a family type (side, what the mods give the goal, their weights) carry which of the tags each mod has; Fire and Cold Resistance, alike for the goal, are then two types when a Fire craft is in the model and one when only a Life craft is. The removal is uniform among the removable mods (not fractured, not on a locked side), so only their number and sides count, and the crafted mods keep their ids.
- **A side swap draws at the level of the beast.** `Reads.draw_levels` makes the family types carry the weight of each mod in the pool of the item at those levels as well, so two families alike at the item's level but not at the beast's are two types (Cold Resistance tier 1 needs level 84: drawn by a level 86 item, not by a level 83 beast). A family that only the beast level's pool holds is told apart by name.
- **The Talisman fractures are the Fracturing Orb's mid-route fracture.** They declare what the orb declares (`fractures`, `pool_weight`, no family by name), so two fractured junk mods of one family type are one state; the recipe for two fractures is a `Finite` of the pairs. The pilot count has no rule for a fracture made mid-route (`EstimateRefused`), and a caller builds the graph without a forecast.
- **The aspect and the Matron** needed no new declaration: the aspect names the family `GrantedSkill`, the Matron the five metamod families and makes the state model the metamods.
- **What the state cannot hold (reported, `OutOfScopeError`).** A goal with a roll limit (`min_roll`) on a mod that one of these crafts adds: the added mod of a Harvest add, a swap target, a side swap's draw and an aspect have a roll of their own, and the compressed result of a `Fixed` part has no roll law (`Abstraction._guard_roll`); the same limit on a mod a pool draw adds (a Chaos Orb) is spread over the law as before. The family-group level (`Coarsening`) and the approximate answer refuse these actions (none declares `Reads.block_invariant`). The pilot count of a model with the Harvest crafts, the aspect, the Matron or the side swaps equals the graph the kernels make (`tests/test_abstraction_harvest_b2.py`, `tests/test_abstraction_beasts.py`).
- **Memory Strands.** These crafts declare no `strand_use` yet (ADR-0041: the rows of the later families are their own tickets), so they are refused on an item with one strand or more; at 0 strands nothing changes.
