# Influence, Harvest and beast crafts in B2 follow the data, then Craft of Exile

B2's single-item additions that are not Eldritch, veiled or catalysts: the influenced Exalted Orbs, the Orb of Dominance, the Harvest add/remove and swap crafts, and the beasts. For each the research ([#86](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/86), [#88](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/88)) left points no source settles. [Grill B2](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/93) settled them by the evidence rule (ADR-0005, [#28](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/28)): where the owner knows, the owner's answer; where nothing speaks, Craft of Exile, and the claim carries `approximate` (ADR-0028).

## Influenced Exalted Orbs and the Orb of Dominance

- Six orbs (Shaper's, Elder's, Crusader's, Hunter's, Redeemer's, Warlord's) add their influence and one mod of it to a rare item with an open slot and item level 68 or more. The mod is drawn only from that influence's mods, through the shared draw (item level, family exclusion, weights, side locks, Attack/Caster blocks). They are refused on **every item that already has an influence, the orb's own included, and on an item with an Eldritch implicit** (the owner has not checked; an optional in-game check may change it, #86 O-01 and O-02), and on fractured and synthesised items. An empty pool refuses the orb.
- The **Orb of Dominance** (body armour, boots, gloves, helmet, with two influenced mods) removes one influenced mod and upgrades another by one tier; a top-tier mod becomes its Elevated version. Both picks are uniform (Craft of Exile, marked approximate). It respects the side locks and ignores the Attack/Caster blocks.
- **Beast influence recipes** add a mod drawn from that influence's pool, as its Exalted Orb would draw it (only the pool is borrowed: the item already has the influence); no source says which mod, so the claim is marked unverified and approximate.
- Randomise Influence and the Awakener's Orb are not here (the first is out of v1; the second is B3).

## Harvest add/remove

Eleven crafts (Fire, Cold, Lightning, Physical, Life, Defence, Chaos, Attack, Caster, Speed, Critical). The craft adds a mod of the tag, then removes **another** one. The addition is drawn **in proportion to the weight** among the tag's mods the item can roll at its item level; the removal is **uniform** among every other non-fractured mod on an unlocked side, crafted mods and metamods included (Craft of Exile, marked approximate). The addition obeys the side locks (open slots on a locked side can be filled) and the Attack/Caster blocks; the removal obeys the locks and ignores the blocks. It needs a non-influenced item and an open slot for the addition. It **works on items with an Eldritch implicit or a fractured mod** (the owner).

## Swaps

- **Resistance swaps** (six, 500 lifeforce of the target colour) change a resistance mod to another element. **The tier is kept and the value is rerolled inside the tier** (the owner); this holds for maximum-resistance swaps too. The required levels of the tiers differ by element, so **a swap into a tier above the item level is refused** (main chat). It respects the side locks.
- **Damage swaps** (six, 500 lifeforce and one Crystallised Rancour) follow Craft of Exile for **which mods qualify**, marked approximate; critical strike multiplier mods are excluded (wiki, since 3.28.0).

## Beasts

- **Wild Bristle Matron** adds one of the five metamods "randomly, only if allowed". Odds: **uniform among the allowed metamods** (main chat, marked approximate). It needs an open slot on the metamod's side and no crafted mod (except `Can have up to 3 Crafted Modifiers`); with the suffixes full and a prefix open the only allowed one is `Suffixes Cannot Be Changed`.
- **Farric Wolf Alpha and Lynx Alpha** add a prefix and remove a random suffix, or the reverse, on rare items. The added mod is gated by the **beast level**, not the item level: a setting, **default 83** when a target names none. "Any rare creature" slots cost 1 chaos each, also a setting.
- **Aspects** (Avian, Cat, Crab, Spider; level 20, or 30 with The Black Mórrígan) are offered only when a target names one. They add a suffix, need an open suffix, and an item holds one aspect; they are **not bench crafts, so they do not use the crafted-mod limit** (Craft of Exile, marked approximate).
- **Talisman fractures**: one fracture on a rare Talisman with at least 4 mods, two with at least 6, not on influenced or fractured items. **Which mods are fractured is uniform** (main chat, marked approximate). The Talisman base reroll is out of v1.

## Amendment: how the influence crafts are built ([#154](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/154))

`actions/influence.py` holds `InfluencedExaltedOrb(influence)`, `OrbOfDominance` and `BeastInfluenceRecipe(influence)`; their claims are `influence.*`, `dominance.*` and `influence_beast.*`. All three are `planner=False` until the compressed state can tell the influenced mods apart and tier an Elevated mod ([#155](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/155)); the contract suite and its kernel check already pass for them.

- **The pool.** An influenced Exalted Orb and a beast recipe draw through the shared pool, restricted to the mods whose deciding spawn tag is the influence's own tag (`harvest_reforge.influence_mod_is_influence_spawn`); the Shaper helmet pool at item level 100 is 11 prefix and 20 suffix records, weights 5,600 and 11,100. The orb is also refused below item level 68 and when no mod is legal (a refusal, nothing consumed).
- **Dominance** lists one result per ordered pair of influenced mods on unlocked sides, each 1/(n(n-1)), as `Finite`: a new mod is at its lowest values, so the value reroll of an Elevated mod is not shown. The next tier of a mod is taken from the mods of its own influence (the first spawn tag the item has decides), so on an Awakener item the Crusader maximum Mana is never the "next tier" of the Elder one. A mod it cannot name the upgrade of is `OutOfScopeError`: an influenced mod with no tier on the item, an ambiguous next tier, a top-tier mod with no Elevated version in the mapping, an influenced item with a fractured mod (no such item exists in the game).
- **The Elevated mapping** is data: `src/crafting_system/data/elevated.json`, made by `python -m crafting_system.data elevated` from Craft of Exile's table `maeven.bmods` read against the pinned export (`data/elevated.py`: by the text of the mods, else by their level and value ranges, else by the one Elevated mod of the family). It holds 206 top-tier mods of the 3.29.3.3 export; the 35 kinds of table entry (207 keys) it cannot link are listed in the file with the reason, because Craft of Exile's table is older than the export. It is for one export; the patch-update skill rebuilds it, and a test fails until it is.
- **Beast recipes** price the named red beast, a Craicic Maw and two rare beasts of any kind (the setting of `beastcraft.any_rare_creature_price`).

Origin: [Grill B2](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/93) (decisions list; owner round 1, point 4, items 3 to 5); [Influence crafts](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/86); [Harvest add/remove/swap and beasts](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/88); ADR-0013, ADR-0028.

## Amendment: the compressed state of the Harvest add, remove and swap crafts and of the beast crafts ([#149](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/149))

The seven kinds (`harvest-add-remove`, `harvest-resistance-swap`, `harvest-damage-swap`, `talisman-fracture`, `aspect-beast`, `wild-bristle-matron`, `beast-side-swap`) are planner kinds now, all `opt_in`: a target names them in `actions:`, because they make a state keep the junk (and the Matron makes it model the metamods), so no other target pays for them. An aspect is offered, on top of that, only for a target that wants it (`Adds.wanted_only`). Every action here answers with a `Finite` (a swap with its `RolledFinite`), so the kernel is the generic one of `Fixed` parts; what #149 added is what the state must keep for each action to be exact (layer 1 against the finished items of the action, `tests/test_abstraction_harvest_b2.py`, `tests/test_abstraction_beasts.py`, `tests/test_abstraction_b2_real.py`). **Amended by #886 (OR-41):** no kind of the beta's scope is `opt_in` any more; every one is offered by default, and the flag is left to the kinds outside the scope (the recombinator, the Foulborn Exalted Orb) and to the user's switch (#323).

- **A swap keeps the tier, so its source families are told apart by mod.** The state forgot which tier of a family a junk mod was (a slot is a family or a family type), so a Lightning Resistance tier 1 and a tier 3 were one slot and the swap refused or answered for the wrong one. `Reads.by_mod` names the families whose slots are the kind `$<mod id>` (the mechanism of a Divine Orb's rolls); a swap names the families of the mods of the item domain that spawn and carry a stat of its claim for either element, 6 families for Fire to Cold resistance and 14 for Fire to Cold damage in 3.29.3.3, and also `families` the same set, so the families a swapped mod would clash with are in it.
- **A Harvest add draws by a tag; the family types carry it.** The new mod is drawn by weight among the mods of the pool with the craft's tag, so a held family matters through the weight and the tag of its mods. `Reads.draw_tags` makes a family type (side, what the mods give the goal, their weights) carry which of the tags each mod has; Fire and Cold Resistance, alike for the goal, are then two types when a Fire craft is in the model and one when only a Life craft is. The removal is uniform among the removable mods (not fractured, not on a locked side), so only their number and sides count, and the crafted mods keep their ids.
- **A side swap draws at the level of the beast.** `Reads.draw_levels` makes the family types carry the weight of each mod in the pool of the item at those levels as well, so two families alike at the item's level but not at the beast's are two types (Cold Resistance tier 1 needs level 84: drawn by a level 86 item, not by a level 83 beast). A family that only the beast level's pool holds is told apart by name.
- **The Talisman fractures are the Fracturing Orb's mid-route fracture.** They declare what the orb declares (`fractures`, `pool_weight`, no family by name), so two fractured junk mods of one family type are one state; the recipe for two fractures is a `Finite` of the pairs. The pilot count has no rule for a fracture made mid-route (`EstimateRefused`), and a caller builds the graph without a forecast.
- **The aspect and the Matron** needed no new declaration: the aspect names the family `GrantedSkill`, the Matron the five metamod families and makes the state model the metamods.
- **What the state cannot hold (reported, `OutOfScopeError`).** A goal with a roll limit (`min_roll`) on a mod that one of these crafts adds: the added mod of a Harvest add, a swap target, a side swap's draw and an aspect have a roll of their own, and the compressed result of a `Fixed` part has no roll law (`Abstraction._guard_roll`); the same limit on a mod a pool draw adds (a Chaos Orb) is spread over the law as before. The family-group level (`Coarsening`) and the approximate answer refuse these actions (none declares `Reads.block_invariant`). The pilot count of a model with the Harvest crafts, the aspect, the Matron or the side swaps equals the graph the kernels make (`tests/test_abstraction_harvest_b2.py`, `tests/test_abstraction_beasts.py`).
- **Memory Strands.** These crafts declare no `strand_use` yet (ADR-0041: the rows of the later families are their own tickets), so they are refused on an item with one strand or more; at 0 strands nothing changes.

## Amendment: the compressed state of the influence crafts ([#155](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/155))

The Orb of Dominance, the influenced Exalted Orbs, the beast recipes and the Awakener's Orb were exact as actions (#154, #213) but a state could not hold what Dominance reads: the kind of a slot was a family, and the families of the data hold ordinary and influence mods together (in 3.29.3.3, 268 of the 358 families that have an influence mod also have ordinary ones: Increased Attack Speed on gloves is ordinary tiers, Elder tiers and Elder attack-and-cast-speed tiers). A slot of the family could stand for a mod of another tier, or for an ordinary one, and the orb answered for the first mod of the family. What #155 adds, none of it a change of the decisions above:

- **`Reads.influenced`** says an action reads which mods are influence mods of the item and which mod each is (Dominance: its next tier, its Elevated version, whether it is one; the Awakener's Orb: which it keeps and whether it clashes with the donor's). The state then makes every influence mod of the item's influences a kind of its own, `$<mod id>`, the kind a swap uses for the families it looks inside (`Reads.by_mod`). The test is the one the actions read, `influence_spawn_test`: the first spawn weight of the mod that the item has is the tag of one of its influences, whatever the weight. The other mods keep the kinds they would have had (Dominance alone counts them by side: `families=frozenset()`, where it declared every family before), and the representative the kernel puts on a rebuilt item for such a kind is never an influence mod. An item with no influence has none, so a model without a reader, and every item without an influence, has the states it had. The influenced Exalted Orb and the beast recipe only put influence mods on an item and declare nothing; the state names the mods they draw when a reader is in the model.
- **A kept mod is read again when the item gets an influence.** Whether a mod is an influence mod depends on the influences of the item that holds it, so a kept slot of a result with other influences than the item given (an Exalted Orb, an Awakener's Orb) is given the kind the new context says (`Abstraction._reread`). It is a no-op for the mods of the data today that are influence mods under the old influences (they stay so under more), and it keeps the state exact for a mod whose Elder tag comes before its default tag.
- **The Elevated tier** is none (ticket #484 removed the stored tier and its constant): an Elevated mod has weight 0 on the influence tag, so no tier table counts it (`tiers_of` lists the mods that can spawn), and the item stores no tier for it, in the item the orb makes or in the item a state rebuilds. A requirement `min_tier` of any value is met by an Elevated mod if its ids name it. An Elevated mod is an influence mod, so it is a kind `$<Elevated id>`, and an Orb of Dominance on it picks it again and leaves the state as it was.
- **Layer 1** passes for the Orb of Dominance (also beside a lock and with an Annulment), the influenced Exalted Orbs, the beast recipes and the Awakener's Orb, on every item they reach together from starts that hold two or three influence mods, a mixed family, an Elevated mod and a lock, for goals that name influence mods and their Elevated versions (`tests/test_abstraction_dominance.py`), and on the real 3.29.3.3 pool of gloves for Dominance, Annulment, both Exalted Orbs and both beast recipes (`tests/test_abstraction_dominance_real.py`). The oracle now gives a drawn mod the tier of the pool of the item it is drawn onto (`abstraction_b2_support.finished_at_tiers`), not its tier on an amulet.
- **Not here.** The kinds stay `planner=False` (the influenced Exalted Orbs: no longer, see the amendment of #818 below): a goal built from a target names no influenced or Elevated mod yet. A goal with a roll limit (`min_roll`) is refused (`OutOfScopeError`) for an Orb of Dominance on an item that holds an Elevated mod, because the orb rolls the values of the Elevated mod it picks afresh (`Finite.rerolled`, `Fixed.rerolled`, named for every Elevated mod it may pick) and the state keeps the contribution of the old roll, as for the kept mod of an Awakener's Orb; the limit on a mod that Dominance puts on the item is refused as for any forced mod. The Awakener's Orb on a real pool rerolls the rest of the item over about sixty families told apart one by one, and is checked on the fixture pool only.

## Amendment: the influenced Exalted Orbs are a planner kind ([#818](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/818))

- **Offered by default.** The six influenced Exalted Orbs are a method of the beta (batch B2, OR-37) and a planner kind, offered by default like every beta method (OR-41). A goal built from a target names an influenced mod as it names any mod, by its family and tier (the target's tier ladder reads the influences of the target's start); "names no influenced mod yet" in "Not here" above no longer holds for them. They go through the one envelope search. The kind declares the item state it reads itself (`reads_item_state_while_not_planner="any"`): the implicits, because the orb is refused on an Eldritch implicit, and the compressed state keeps them. An item class with no influence key (a jewel, a flask) or an unknown base lists no influenced Exalted Orb and tells it as a model gap (`listing_gap`), from the one function that reads the key (`influence_tag`).
- **The lower bound.** The orb reads the implicits and changes the influences (part of the state's flags), so it leaves the start's shape: the offers' floors class it TERMINAL (`planner/_offer_kinds`), its floor is its price and Wald's argument for `rho` is cut at its first use. Tests on hand-worked numbers through the planner's entry: `tests/test_planner_influenced_exalts.py`.
- **Still not planner kinds.** The Orb of Dominance and the beast influence recipes (both B2, in the beta) kept `planner=False` here; their tickets are named in #818's PR (Dominance: no longer, see the amendment of #1195 below).

## Amendment: the Orb of Dominance is a planner kind ([#1195](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/1195))

- **Offered by default.** The Orb of Dominance is a method of the beta (batch B2, OR-37) and a planner kind, offered by default (OR-41), through the one envelope search. The catalog is asked once, for the start item, so the orb is listed for a body armour, boots, gloves or helmet that is not corrupted, holds no fractured mod and has an influence or an item level of 68 or more (an influenced Exalted Orb can then give it one, and its influenced mods are drawn like any other); the rarity and the count of influenced mods change on the way and stay the action's refusals. The Elevated mapping is the committed one (`data/elevated.json`, for the export it was made for).
- **What a target can ask.** A goal names the next tier of an influenced mod by its family and tier, as any mod, and an Elevated mod by `mod:` alone: it has no natural tier, so a tier leaf (`min_tier`, or the top tier of OR-1) reads the natural ladder and is not met by an Elevated mod, as stated above. Whether a tier leaf should also accept the Elevated version of the mods it accepts is a reading of the target, filed as #1225. A roll limit (`min_roll`) stays out of the orb's model as "Not here" says: where the orb would put a new mod a leaf limits, or pick an Elevated mod under a goal with a roll limit, the kernel raises `OutOfScopeError`. That gap holds at some states of a group the envelope search judges together and not at others, so the search would refuse the whole route; the orb therefore declares it (`Adds.rolls_unfollowed`), and for a goal with any roll limit the offers leave the orb out and the answer says it as a model gap of the listing (`planner/_offers`). The roll law of a new or rerolled fixed-result mod that lifts it is #993.
- **The lower bound.** The orb reads the junk and the influenced mods (`Reads.influenced`) and adds a mod it does not name (`Adds.named` None), so the offers' floors class it TERMINAL (`planner/_offer_kinds`): its floor is its price, pbar 1, and Wald's argument for `rho` is cut at its first use. A tighter floor is exact: the orb keeps the flags (rarity, corruption, influences) and the fractured mods (an influenced item holds none), so it keeps the start's shape, and the only mod it adds is the upgrade of an influenced mod held (its next tier in its own influence's chain, or its Elevated version), so pbar = 1 when such an upgrade of an influence mod of the shape's influences can pass a leaf of U and 0 otherwise, as for a bench craft. It is not built here: the orb's price (329.2 Chaos in the 3.29 week-one file) is above the TERMINAL floors already offered where it is listed with no influence (the influenced Exalted Orbs); it is #1226. Tests on hand-worked numbers through the planner's entry: `tests/test_planner_dominance.py`.
- **Still not a planner kind.** The beast influence recipes kept `planner=False` here (#1196; no longer, see the amendment of #1196 below).

## Amendment: the beast influence recipes are a planner kind ([#1196](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/1196))

- **Offered by default.** The six beast influence recipes ("Add a Mod to a <influence> Item") are a method of the beta (batch B2, OR-37) and a planner kind, offered by default (OR-41), through the one envelope search. The catalog is asked once, for the start item, so the recipes are listed for an item that is not corrupted and holds no fractured mod: the recipe of every influence where the item may gain one on the way (fewer than two influences and item level 68 or more: an influenced Exalted Orb gives one, an Awakener's Orb a second), else the recipes of the influences it holds. The rarity and the influences held change on the way and stay the action's refusals. An item class with no influence key lists none and tells it as a model gap, as the influenced Exalted Orbs do. The answer names the approximate claim the recipe rests on (`influence_beast.adds_a_mod_of_that_influence`: no source says which mod it adds; it borrows the pool of the influence's Exalted Orb). Its beasts are priced from the price file like any input; the 3.29 week-one file prices them at about 1 Chaos each (the stand-in prices of the B2 beasts are #813's).
- **The lower bound: an addition.** The recipe keeps every mod and adds one mod drawn by weight from the influence's pool less the families held, over the sides with room together, as an Exalted Orb draws; it reads the junk and every family, not the pool weights. Its class declares that draw for a shape that holds the influence (`Action.added_draw`, with `AddedDraw.together`, which says the draw is over every open side at once and not on one of them: the bound of one added mod already ranges over both), so the offers' floors class it ADD there, with `declared_add_bound`; the contract suite compares the declaration with the built outcomes (`check_added_draw`). A shape without the influence refuses it at every state (the influences are part of the shape), and its class declares nothing there: it stays TERMINAL, its floor is its price (4 Chaos in the 3.29 week-one file) and Wald's argument is cut at its first use. Tests on hand-worked numbers through the planner's entry: `tests/test_planner_beast_influence.py`.
- **The compressed state.** The recipe reads no influenced mod (`Reads.influenced` False), so it is not asked whether it treats the mods of a group alike (`Action.influence_alike`, #1238): it reads the families, and the mods of a group are one family on one side, so its draw and its refusal are the same whichever of them a slot holds.

## Amendment: the beast influence recipes draw as an Exalted Orb ([#1538](https://github.com/Rasslabsya4el/crafting-system/issues/1538))

- **The source.** Maxroll's Beastcrafting Guide (`https://maxroll.gg/poe/crafting/beastcrafting`, dated 2025-06-13, updated for 3.26), section "Influence Slam": "An influence slam adds a modifier to an influenced item. It is functionally the same as an Exalted Orb. It does not guarantee an influenced modifier." No source found says otherwise (the research comment on #1538 lists the eight tried). It replaces the default of grill B2 (the influence's Exalted Orb's pool) that the decision above and the amendment of #1196 describe.
- **The draw.** A recipe of an influence, on a rare item that holds that influence, adds one mod as a plain Exalted Orb does on that item: from the item's whole pool (its base's mods and the mods of its influences, each at its weight), with no strand cut (beast crafts never cut). The influence's mod therefore comes with its weight's share of the whole pool, not always. The class declares that draw for a shape that holds the influence (`added_draw`), so the offers' floors still class it ADD there, and it is no longer an offer "with a pool of its own" that the growth holds back to its later group (`planner/_growth.py`).
- **Still approximate.** One secondary source (a single guide), so `influence_beast.adds_a_mod_of_that_influence` keeps `approximate`; the confirmation is a line on the owner's in-game list #182.
