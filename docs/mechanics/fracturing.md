# Fracturing Orb

Craft of Exile exposes a fracturing action. Its simulator marks one eligible modifier as fractured; the exact eligibility and outcome distribution are not accepted yet. The item must retain a source-backed rule for which modifier classes can be selected and what happens when no eligible modifier exists.

Source: S3 `package.js`, fracturing dispatch. Confidence: `reimplemented-from-CoE`. Probabilities and base restrictions remain unsourced.
