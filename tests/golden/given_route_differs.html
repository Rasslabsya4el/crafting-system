A Harvest damage swap does not take a mod from a side that is locked by Prefixes or Suffixes Cannot Be Changed, as the resistance swap does not. Source: the wiki's table for the resistance swap; the damage swap is taken by analogy.
When several mods of the item qualify for a Harvest swap, the swap changes one of them chosen equally. Source: Craft of Exile's code only; not checked in the game for 3.29.

How the answer was found
The search expanded only what could matter (it weighed the actions on 199 of the 351 items it met along the way and built 385 of the 687 actions offered there: an action it did not build costs more, even at its best case, than the one it kept). The plan is the cheapest among the methods offered. The cut is exact: nothing was left out for being unlikely or by a tolerance, and the mean cost is that of the best plan.
