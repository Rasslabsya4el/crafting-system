A Harvest augment adds a mod with the craft's tag, drawn in proportion to the mods' weights. Source: Craft of Exile's code only; not checked in the game for 3.29.
A Harvest augment removes one mod chosen equally among those that are not fractured, not on a locked side and not the new one. Source: Craft of Exile's code only; not checked in the game for 3.29.
A tagged Harvest reforge is refused when no mod with the chosen tag fits a free slot of the item, for instance when the only free slots are on a side with no such mod. Source: the wiki's sentence about the mod pool; no source says what happens with a full or locked side.
The list of mods that a Harvest damage swap accepts as a source is the planner's own choice. Source: no source lists them, and Craft of Exile has no damage swap.
A Harvest swap does not take a fractured mod, and the planner also does not take a crafted (bench) mod, which has no tier to keep. Source: the wiki for fractured mods; the crafted case is the planner's own choice.
A Harvest damage swap does not take a mod from a side that is locked by Prefixes or Suffixes Cannot Be Changed, as the resistance swap does not. Source: the wiki's table for the resistance swap; the damage swap is taken by analogy.
When several mods of the item qualify for a Harvest swap, the swap changes one of them chosen equally. Source: Craft of Exile's code only; not checked in the game for 3.29.

The plan is the cheapest among every method offered, searched in rounds. Each round held the methods of the rounds before it, so the last one held every method.
The given route's methods were searched from the first round: Chaos Orb (the given route's actions were offered from the first round on, and every round held them). The first round finished: no plan made only of its methods, the route's among them, costs less than the plan shown, so the route costs no less than it.
How the answer was found
The search expanded only what could matter (it looked at the actions of 199 of the 351 states it found and built 566 of the 885 actions offered at them: an action it did not build costs more, even at its best case, than the one it kept). The plan is the cheapest among the methods offered. The cut is exact: nothing was left out for being unlikely or by a tolerance, and the mean cost is that of the best plan.
What a state line shows
A state lists the other mods only as a count per side (the model keeps the mods the target does not ask for by kind, not by name). The odds and costs count every one of them.
