The plan is the cheapest among every method offered, searched in rounds. Each round held the methods of the rounds before it, so the last one held every method.
How the answer was found
The search expanded only what could matter (it looked at the actions of 1 of the 2 states it found and built 1 of the 3 actions offered at them: an action it did not build costs more, even at its best case, than the one it kept). The plan is the cheapest among the methods offered. The cut is exact: nothing was left out for being unlikely or by a tolerance, and the mean cost is that of the best plan.
What a state line shows
A state does not list the other mods (no offered action reads the mods the target does not ask for, so the model does not track them). The odds and costs do not depend on them: no offered action reads them.
