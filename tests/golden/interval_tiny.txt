Approximate inputs (claims that no other source settles):
  How many mods a recombination keeps on a side depends only on how many mods the two inputs hold on that side together; the planner uses the guide's table. Source: a player guide's measurements in 3.25 (217 to 424 events per row), largely the same in 3.26.
      spread: the guide's table is used; the wiki's rounding of the same table differs by up to 2 points in a row
  The wiki gives the same table of kept mods per side, rounded differently; the planner reads it as a second table to see how much the difference matters. Source: the wiki's rounding of the same player measurements.
      spread: the guide's table is used; the wiki's rounding of the same table differs by up to 2 points in a row
  An exclusive mod (crafted, essence-only, or one its own input could not roll) is drawn as if its weight were 1000. Source: the guide author's assumption; the game data gives such mods weight 0.
  A recombination gives a normal item with no mod, a magic item with at most one mod per side, and a rare item when a side holds two or more. Source: Craft of Exile's code, which matches the guide's results.
  Which eligible mods a recombination picks depends on their weight, a higher weight being more likely, but the formula is not published; the planner fits one. Source: a fit to 68 player observations with weights from 250 to 1500; no source gives the formula.
      spread: the planner is run at 1; its cost is read at 0.4, 0.7, 1, 1.3, 1.6
  A recombination fills the prefixes or the suffixes first with equal chance. Source: measured in 3.25; 3.26 reports it no longer holds when one side holds only exclusive mods.
  One recombination costs between 25,000 and 250,000 Dust without a junk craft; the planner uses 100,000. Source: player reports from at least nine authors, 2025-03 to 2026-03; no formula is published.
      spread: the planner uses 100,000; reports range from 25,000 to 250,000 (Thaumaturgic Dust per combine)
  In 3.25 the Gold of a recombination rose in a straight line with the number of mods on the two inputs, about 323 per mod, and did not depend on their tiers. Source: one author's test with two wands in 3.25 at one item level band.
  One recombination costs between 8,000 and 20,000 Gold; the planner uses 12,000. Source: three player reports from 2025-11 to 2026-03, two of them second-hand; no formula is published.
      spread: the planner uses 12,000; reports range from 8,000 to 20,000 (Gold per combine)
  A recombination with an influenced input costs 1.5 times the Dust and 1.5 times the Gold. Source: the 3.25 sheet line does not say Dust or Gold; applying it to both is the planner's default.
  Crafting a low-tier mod on each input before a recombination lowers its Dust; the planner takes a factor of 0.5, the least discount any report gives. Source: player reports, a factor of at least 2 and in one case 8.7.
      spread: the planner uses 0.5; reports range from 0.11 to 0.5 (factor on the Dust when each input carries a junk craft)
